# How to review the Telugu, Hindi or Kannada texts

Thank you for helping. You are checking the words of **Stack19**, a WhatsApp and business app for shops, builders and schools in India. A computer wrote the first version of every text in your language. Your job is to make it sound the way a shop owner would really write it.

## What you have
- A sheet for your language: one row per text, in a file ending **.csv** (open it in Excel, Google Sheets or Numbers) and the same list as a printable page ending **.html** (open it in any browser and print it).
- The columns: **key** (a label, leave it alone), **English** (the original), **current draft** (the computer's text), **corrected** (yours), **comment** (yours), **where it appears (page)**.

## What to do
- Read **current draft** next to **English**. If it is right and natural, **leave "corrected" empty**. That means "fine".
- If a word or sentence is wrong, stiff or too bookish, write the full better text in **corrected**. Write the whole text, not just the changed word.
- Use the words people really say. Words like quote, order, follow-up, payment, WhatsApp, message, settings stay as people say them, in your script. Avoid heavy, formal or government-form words.
- Short sentences. Polite everyday "you" (Telugu *మీరు*, Hindi *आप*, Kannada *ನೀವು*).
- Use **comment** for anything you want us to know: "this word means something else here", "a customer would say it differently", "not sure".

## Three rules that protect the app
- **Keep anything in curly brackets exactly as it is**, for example `{name}`, `{n}`, `{brand}`, `{privacy_email}`. The app puts a name, a number or an address there. Move it where it fits your sentence, but do not translate, change or delete it.
- Keep names as they are: **Stack19**, **Agent OWL**, **WhatsApp**, **GST**, **UPI**, plan names and business names in sample chats.
- Do not add promises or numbers that are not in the English (no "guaranteed", no percentages, no "used by many").

## The legal pages
The Privacy Policy and the data-deletion page are legal texts. Keep the meaning exactly. If the English says "within 7 days", yours says 7 days. If you are unsure, write it in **comment**, do not guess.

## Sending it back
- Save the sheet as **CSV UTF-8** (in Excel: Save As, "CSV UTF-8"; in Google Sheets: File, Download, CSV). Do not change the **key** or **current draft** columns, and do not delete rows or columns.
- Or print the **.html** page, write your corrections by hand, and send photos or the pages; we will type them in.
- Send it to the person who gave you the sheet. It takes about two to three hours for the whole sheet; you can do it in parts and tell us which rows you finished.
