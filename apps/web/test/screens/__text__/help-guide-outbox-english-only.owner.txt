# Outbox: why a message waits
Every message has a status and a reason.
{label="What to do"}
## What to do
- 1 Open Outbox. The tabs are Waiting, Scheduled, Sent and Blocked, each with a count.
- 2 A message waits for you when it is about price, a promise or a complaint.
- 3 It is held for the morning when sending would fall in quiet hours.
- 4 Blocked means a rule stopped it (for example the customer asked us to stop, or has not agreed to WhatsApp messages). Open the row to read the reason.
