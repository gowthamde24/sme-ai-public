{label="Quiet hours"}
## Quiet hours
21:00 – 09:00 India time. Nothing goes out in these hours; what is due waits until 09:00. You cannot change this yet.
{label="Visit reminders"}
## Visit reminders
These are the standard settings. Save your own to replace them. Agent OWL reminds customers of a confirmed visit that has not started. It never sends in quiet hours, and only works while sending is on for the system and for this business.
