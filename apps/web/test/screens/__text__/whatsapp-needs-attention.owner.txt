# Connect WhatsApp
Link the business's WhatsApp number, so messages to customers can go out and their replies come in. Nothing is sent until you approve it.
{label="Your WhatsApp number"}
## Your WhatsApp number
Needs attention The system does not have the access key named SHOP_TOKEN. The person who runs the system must add it. Until then nothing can be sent. Last changed: Oct 6, 2026, 8:30 AM (1 hour ago) On a test or demo system, messages go to a stand-in on that machine, never to customers. Ask the person who runs the system which kind this is.

## The number's details
- Get your WhatsApp Business account ID and phone number ID from your Meta business account.
- Type them below with the name of the access key, and save.
- The person who runs the system adds the access key under that name.
- Switch the number on.

## Message templates
A template is a message that WhatsApp has approved in advance. It is the only kind that can be sent to a customer whose 24 hours are over. Only an approved one is ever used.
