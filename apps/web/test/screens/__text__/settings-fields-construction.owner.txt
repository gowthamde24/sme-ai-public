Fields hold the extra details you keep about your customers and your products. Your kind of business comes with some; you can add your own.
{label="Customers and contacts"}
## Customers and contacts
2 of 20 in use
- Customer type Choice From your business type
- Credit days agreed Number From your business type
{label="Catalogue items"}
## Catalogue items
15 of 20 in use
- Product type Choice From your business type Required
- Size (for example 200x100 mm) Text From your business type
- Thickness (mm) Choice From your business type
- Shape or pattern Text From your business type
- Colour Text From your business type
- Finish Text From your business type
- Grade or strength (for example M30) Text From your business type
- Selling unit Choice From your business type Required
- Pieces per sq ft Number From your business type
- Pieces per pallet Number From your business type
- Minimum order Number From your business type
- Stock Choice From your business type
- Lead days (made to order) Number From your business type
- GST rate % (from your accountant) Number From your business type
- HSN code (from your accountant) Text From your business type
{label="Add a field"}
## Add a field
Do not use fields for health, religion, caste, income, Aadhaar or other private details.
