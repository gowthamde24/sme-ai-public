# Rules
Standing rules for new enquiries, calling hours and how many tries.
## Calling hours and tries
Call between 9 am and 7 pm , Mon, Tue, Wed, Thu, Fri, Sat . If nobody answers, try 3 times , 18 hours apart. Then it sends a WhatsApp message and stops. A lead that comes after hours is called the next morning at 9:30 am. Nothing is sent at night. When someone is interested, a team member is told at once, and reminded after 15 minutes if nobody has called back. Anyone who says no, or sends STOP, is never contacted again. Only your own contacts are called. [Change the calling hours](/app/tenants/22222222-2222-2222-2222-222222222222/settings?section=assistant)
