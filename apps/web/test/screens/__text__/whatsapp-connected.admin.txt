# Connect WhatsApp
Link the business's WhatsApp number, so messages to customers can go out and their replies come in. Nothing is sent until you approve it.

## Message templates
A template is a message that WhatsApp has approved in advance. It is the only kind that can be sent to a customer whose 24 hours are over. Only an approved one is ever used.
