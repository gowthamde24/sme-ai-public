{label="When a customer pushes back"}
### When a customer pushes back
- When they say: too costly, price is high I understand. Our team can share the details and explain what is included.
{label="Follow-ups"}
### Follow-ups
- No answer · 30 minutes after · Phone calls
- Interested · 5 minutes after · WhatsApp messages
{label="Rules that always apply"}
### Rules that always apply
- The assistant says it is an AI assistant on every call.
- It states only facts you approved, and never a price you did not approve.
- It stops when someone says no or sends STOP.
These belong to your business type and to the platform. They cannot be changed here.
