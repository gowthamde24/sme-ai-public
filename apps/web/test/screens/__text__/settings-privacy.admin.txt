## Delete my account
This deletes your account and your sign-in. What you typed into a business's records stays with that business, and its audit trail keeps only an anonymous id of you. Nothing happens at once. There are 7 days to change your mind, and you can cancel until the day. Type your e-mail address (owner@example.test) to confirm.
