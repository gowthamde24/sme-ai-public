## Try it before you go live
- Press Talk now and speak as a customer would.
- Or press Call my phone to get a test call on a phone from the test list.
- If an answer is wrong, press Teach this and write what it should have said. It goes into Knowledge and you can undo it.
Nothing is sent to customers from Practice.
