# Suppression keys
Your role: owner A suppression key lets the system remember that a person asked not to be contacted, even after their details are erased. A contact without a key can never receive a follow-up draft. Contacts made before the key was set up, or straight through the database, have none until you record them here. Nothing is sent to anyone, and no key or address is shown on this page.
