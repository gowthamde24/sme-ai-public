## Automatic replies
Off When a customer writes to you on WhatsApp, your assistant can answer by itself, in the customer's language, using only the facts you approved. Nothing is invented. It always follows these rules:
- It answers only within 24 hours of the customer's message.
- A price, a discount or a promise always waits for you.
- If it is unsure, the customer is upset, or asks for a person, it hands over to you.
A reply goes out only while automatic sending is on in your message settings; otherwise it waits for your approval. Turning it on is your approval of these rules, for every message.

## Listening in and taking over
Off When this is on, you or an Admin can, during a live call, listen to the caller and speak to them from the app. The assistant stops while you speak and carries on when you hand the call back.
- Nothing is recorded: no sound of the caller or of you is kept.
- Every change of this switch, and every time someone listens in, whispers, takes over or hangs up, is written to the audit log with who and when. The words said are never in it.
- The assistant itself can never change this switch; only you can, here.
- The caller is not told when you join: the assistant's voice fades out and you speak. (A short spoken announcement is possible; it is off for now and the owner decides.)
It is off until you turn it on, for each business separately.
