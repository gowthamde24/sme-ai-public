# Integrations
Bring in a price list or a spreadsheet, connect WhatsApp, send your books to your accountant and see visits in your calendar. E-mail and UPI payments are not built yet.
- Available
## Price list (CSV)
Upload your price list so quotes are priced from it by fixed rules. [Import price list (CSV)](/app/tenants/22222222-2222-2222-2222-222222222222/price-list)
- Available
## WhatsApp
Connect the business's WhatsApp number. Messages you approve go out from it, and customers' replies come in as chats. [Connect WhatsApp](/app/tenants/22222222-2222-2222-2222-222222222222/integrations/whatsapp)
- Available
## Calendar
See your store visits in your phone's calendar. [Open Data settings](/app/tenants/22222222-2222-2222-2222-222222222222/settings?section=data)
- Available
## Accounting
Send your sales and payments to Tally, Vyapar or Excel for your accountant. [Open Data settings](/app/tenants/22222222-2222-2222-2222-222222222222/settings?section=data)
- Not available yet
## E-mail
Would let the app send quotes by e-mail. Not built.
- Not available yet
## UPI payments
Would confirm payments automatically. Today you record them.
