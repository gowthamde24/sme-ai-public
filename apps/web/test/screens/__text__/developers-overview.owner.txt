## Make your first request
Ask who you are, then try a call with dry_run on: everything is checked and nobody is dialled.
- 3
## Get results by webhook
Every call outcome, summary and the details it captured are sent to your address. [Webhooks](/app/tenants/22222222-2222-2222-2222-222222222222/developers?tab=hooks)
Base address
https://api.stack19.in/v1
Sign in with
Authorization: Bearer YOUR_KEY
Add an Idempotency-Key header to every request that writes: sending it twice never makes two calls.
{role=group label="Language"} [cURL](/app/tenants/22222222-2222-2222-2222-222222222222/developers?tab=overview&lang=curl current=true) [Node](/app/tenants/22222222-2222-2222-2222-222222222222/developers?tab=overview&lang=node) [Python](/app/tenants/22222222-2222-2222-2222-222222222222/developers?tab=overview&lang=python)
curl https://api.stack19.in/v1/me \ -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_KEY" # a call that checks everything and dials nobody curl -X POST https://api.stack19.in/v1/calls \ -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_KEY" \ -H "Idempotency-Key: first-try-001" \ -d '{"to":"+919800000000","job":"enquiry_call","dry_run":true}'
