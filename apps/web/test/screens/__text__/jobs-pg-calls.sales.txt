# Calls
Every call your assistant answered or made
summary: Before you start calling Read the five rules
- 1 Only call people who agreed. People who asked about your business, or said yes to calls. Not bought or collected lists.
- 2 We fix the calling hours. Calls go out between 10 am and 9 pm. Outside these hours they wait.
- 3 Do-not-call is automatic. When someone asks you to stop, they are added to your do-not-call list and are never called again.
- 4 Your business is the caller. You are responsible for who is called and what is said. We place the call for you.
- 5 Phone rules may apply. The national do-not-disturb list and phone company rules can stop some calls, mostly calls about offers.
