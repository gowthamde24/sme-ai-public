{label="How it decides"}
### Calls straight away
Good fits for what you have in stock. The first 10 calls of every new search still wait for you.
### Asks you first
Middling fits, shops where your stock is low, and the first 10 calls of every new search.
### Skips
Poor fits, competitors, very large shops, shops with no phone, shops you contacted in the last 90 days, and anyone you asked never to call.
No real call is ever placed from here: a call is a practice call (simulated) for now.
