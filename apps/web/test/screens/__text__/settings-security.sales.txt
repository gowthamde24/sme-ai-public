## Two-step sign-in (optional)
A second code from an authenticator app protects your account if your password or Google account is ever stolen. It is your choice. We recommend it for the Owner. 2-step sign-in is not set up yet. [Turn on two-step sign-in (authenticator app)](/app/security)
{label="Sign out of all devices"}
## Sign out of all devices
Ends every sign-in of your account: this browser, your phone, anywhere you left it open. Use it if a device was lost or someone else may know your password. Other devices are signed out within the hour.
