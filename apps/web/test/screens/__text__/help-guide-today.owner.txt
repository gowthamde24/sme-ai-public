# Today: what needs you
Your day on one screen.
{label="What to do"}
## What to do
- 1 Open Today. The line at the top says how many things need you.
- 2 Read each card under Needs you. It says who it is for and why it waits.
- 3 Press Approve, change the words with Edit, or say no. Nothing on these cards goes out until you decide.
- 4 Below, Done while you slept shows what your assistant already handled.
