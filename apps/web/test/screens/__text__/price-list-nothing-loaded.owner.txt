## Load a price list
{role=note} A price list is a file of your products and their prices. Check it first: nothing is saved until you press save, and nothing is sent to anyone. Prices in a quote always come from the price list in force on the day, never from a person or an assistant.
form:
Choose a CSV file
<file label="Choose a CSV file" accept=.csv,text/csv,text/plain>
Or paste the file's text
<textarea name=csv label="Or paste the file's text" placeholder="sku,name,unit_price,moq,tax_bps">
Columns: sku, name, unit_price, moq, tax_bps and, optionally, min_qty_1, price_1 up to min_qty_5, price_5 . Prices are in rupees (4200 or 1,200.50). Every product code (sku) must already be a product in your catalog. In plain words: sku is the product's code, unit_price is the price in rupees, moq is the smallest quantity you sell, and tax_bps is the GST: 500 means ₹5 of tax on every ₹100 (1800 means ₹18). Example, one product on each line: SAREE-001,Maroon Kanchi pattu,38500,1,500
