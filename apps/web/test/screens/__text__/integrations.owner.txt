# Integrations
Bring in a price list or a spreadsheet, connect WhatsApp, send your books to your accountant and see visits in your calendar. E-mail and UPI payments are not built yet.
-
## Price list (CSV)
Upload your price list so quotes are priced from it by fixed rules. Available [Import price list (CSV)](/app/tenants/22222222-2222-2222-2222-222222222222/price-list)
-
## WhatsApp
Connect the business's WhatsApp number. Messages you approve go out from it, and customers' replies come in as chats. Available [Connect WhatsApp](/app/tenants/22222222-2222-2222-2222-222222222222/integrations/whatsapp)
-
## Calendar
See your store visits in your phone's calendar. Available [Open Data settings](/app/tenants/22222222-2222-2222-2222-222222222222/settings?section=data)
-
## Accounting
Send your sales and payments to Tally, Vyapar or Excel for your accountant. Available [Open Data settings](/app/tenants/22222222-2222-2222-2222-222222222222/settings?section=data)
-
## E-mail
Would let the app send quotes by e-mail. Not built. Not available yet
-
## UPI payments
Would confirm payments automatically. Today you record them. Not available yet
