## How calling works
Calls go out on Mon, Tue, Wed, Thu, Fri, Sat, from 09:00 to 19:00. A lead that arrives after hours is called the next morning at 09:30. Nothing is sent at night. If nobody answers, your assistant tries up to 3 times, about 18 hours apart, then sends a WhatsApp message and stops. When someone is interested, a team member is told at once and reminded after 15 minutes if nobody has called back. Anyone who says no, or sends STOP, is never contacted again. Only your own contacts are called. [Change the calling hours](/app/tenants/22222222-2222-2222-2222-222222222222/settings?section=assistant)
