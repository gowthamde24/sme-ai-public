## How it talks to you
Choose how your assistant sounds and speaks when it talks to you: in Talk live, in chat and in everything it tells you. Customers are not affected. These choices are only yours. Everyone on your team chooses their own. Test version: your choices are kept in this browser only, and a sample shows its words, not a voice. Using defaults: Respectful, normal speed.
