## 1. What it is
After a call or a lead event, the result is written into the CRM or sheet the business already uses: find or make the person, and (for a call) add a note. It is the outbound half of the connectors plan (inbound lead intake is `lead_sources`). Only data we already hold about the business's **own** leads and calls leaves; consent and do-not-call are untouched (nothing is dialled, nothing is sent to a customer). A test lead, a simulated or sandbox call, and a person who has been erased are **never** sent (`skipped`).

- **Who:** Owner and Admin (everyone else `403`). Changes need the second factor (`403 mfa_required`).
- **Keys:** only in the workspace-secrets store (Secret Manager `sme-ws-<workspace>-crm-<connection>`, ONE JSON value of the keys), written and removed by the API. Never in a table, a log, an audit row, an error or an answer; never shown again (re-enter to replace; a replace bumps `secret_version`). Needs `WORKSPACE_SECRETS` (`503 crm_push_unavailable` without it).
- **One connection per tool per workspace.** Field mapping, events, on/off are per connection.
- **Events** (the outbound webhook names, from the same triggers): `call.completed`, `call.missed`, `lead.created`, `lead.updated`, `job.outcome`. Default on a new connection: all but `lead.updated`.
- **Record** (our fields): `name, phone, email, source, outcome, summary, captured` (the answers the assistant took down), `duration` (seconds), `call_id`; for tools that keep one row per event also `event` and `ref` (the event's id). The lead's own contact only; never a transcript; never a third party's number.
- **Mapping:** per field, the tool's own field name, or `@note` (into the note/activity text), or `""` (do not send). Names: letters, digits and `_ .:/()-`, at most 60 characters. Each tool has a default mapping (`default_mapping` in the card).
- **Idempotency:** one delivery row per (connection, event, call/lead id) (`unique (connection_id, dedupe_key)`; for `lead.updated` and `job.outcome` per minute), so the same event twice is one push. In the tool: contacts are found first (email, then phone) and updated; row tools upsert on the `Stack19 ref` column (Airtable, Notion query, the Apps Script looks at the last 500 rows); every note ends with `Ref: evt_<id>`.
- **Retries:** the outbound-webhook schedule, 1 min, 5 min, 30 min, 2 h, 6 h, then **dead**. A record the tool refused as invalid (`rejected`), too big, or a setting that does not check (`config`) is dead at once. A connection with a dead push is **failing** until one push works; Today's **Needs you** shows `crm_push_failing` (target `connectors`) to the Owner and Admin. "Try again" requeues the dead pushes of the last 7 days.
- **Limits:** record at most 256 KB, answer read at most 256 KB, 5 s connect, 10 s read, at most 4 calls per push, 10 pushes per worker tick. Log kept 30 days. Closed result words only (`created, updated, noted, appended, skipped`; failures `auth, rejected, not_found, rate_limited, server, bad_answer, timeout, refused, tls, redirect, blocked, dns, too_large, no_secret, config`): the other side's words are never kept.
- **SSRF:** every address passes `webhooks_out.ssrf` (https only, a real host name, no IP literal, port 443 or 1024+, every resolved address public; the connection goes to the checked address; no redirect is followed). Hosts the business types are also held to the vendor's own domains (LeadSquared `api[-xx00].leadsquared.com`, Freshsales `*.myfreshworks.com` / `*.freshsales.io`, Sell.Do `*.sell.do`, Bitrix24 `*.bitrix24.<tld>`, Google `script.google.com`, Pipedrive `<name>.pipedrive.com`).
- **Kill switch:** `CRM_PUSH=off` (default) hides the routes and stops the worker; a connection can also be paused (`active: false`); disconnecting deletes the connection, its log and its keys. Zoho CRM and Salesforce stay **Needs setup by us** until the owner steps in section 5 are done.
- **Web address (Zapier, Make, n8n, Pabbly, your own server):** not a connector of this module: it is the outbound webhook path (`docs/contracts/webhooks-out.md`, `WEBHOOKS_OUT`). The Connectors page shows it as a card that opens that form.

## 2. Routes (`/v1/tenants/{tenant_id}/connectors/crm`)
| | |
|---|---|
| `GET` | `{items: [Card], events: [{event, description}], available}` (`available` false: no secret store) |
| `PUT /{type}` `{credentials: {...}, settings?: {...}, mapping?, events?}` | `{connection}`. `credentials` has exactly the card's keys (no spaces, 600 characters), `settings` exactly its settings. `409 needs_setup_by_us` for Zoho and Salesforce; `422 credentials_invalid / settings_invalid / mapping_invalid / validation_error`. |
| `PATCH /{type}` `{mapping?, events?, active?}` | `{connection}` |
| `DELETE /{type}` | `{deleted: true}` |
| `POST /{type}/test` | `{ok, reason}`: a harmless **read** of the other tool (nothing is written there); the answer is kept on the connection (`last_test`). Airtable and Notion also check that every mapped column exists (`config`). |
| `GET /{type}/deliveries?before=&limit=` | `{items, next_before}`: status, attempts, result, closed error word, response code; never a body |
| `POST /{type}/retry` | `{requeued}` |

`Card = {type, label, state: "connected"|"not_connected"|"needs_setup", needs_setup, credentials[], settings[], targets[], our_fields[], default_mapping, uses_notes, note_value, setup_steps[], script?, connection}`. `connection = {id, type, config, mapping, events, active, secret_version, last_test, health: {status, failing_since, dead_letters_7d, last_delivered_at}}`: no key.
