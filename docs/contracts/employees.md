# Contract: AI employees (C1 -> C2), 26 Nov 2026
Status: built locally on branch `c1/ai-employees` (migrations 20261231992750, 992760, 992770). API flag `EMPLOYEES` (404 on every route while off; web flag name is C2's). Design: `reports/ai-employees-design.md`.

A **workspace has one DEFAULT employee** (today's business assistant; its persona stays in "My assistant": name, voices, speed, tone) and, on a paid plan, up to four more (a trial has only the default). Each other employee has its own name, role, responsibilities, instructions, languages, voice per language, opening line, hours, what it may do alone, never-say words, and may answer a phone number. The owner's Today assistant is NOT an employee.

## Routes (prefix `/v1/tenants/{t}/employees`; every member reads; Owner or Admin write, with the second factor when the authenticator is on)
* `GET ""` -> `{employees: [Employee], limit}` (`limit` 1 on a trial, 5 paid; the default employee counts; archived ones are not listed). The default employee is first.
* `POST ""` `{id (the web mints it once per opening; the same id again is a retry), role_code, name 1..24}` -> 201 an Employee with `status: "draft"` and the role's starting `responsibilities`.
* `GET /{id}` -> Employee.  `PUT /{id}` a PATCH (what is left out is kept; explicit `null` clears `role_title`, `extra_instructions`, `style`, `speed`, `hours`). Editable: `name`, `role_code`, `role_title` (3..60), `responsibilities` (ids), `extra_instructions` (<= 1,500), `never_say` (<= 30 words of <= 60), `languages`, `auto_switch_language`, `allow_english_mixing`, `style` (friendly|respectful|quick|casual), `speed` (0.7..1.3), `opening_line` ({language: 10..300 chars}), `autonomy`, `ask_first`, `hours`. Tags and angle brackets are removed from every text before it is stored.
* `POST /{id}/status` `{status: "on" | "paused" | "archived"}`. A draft needs a role title or at least one responsibility before it can be switched on. Archiving frees its number. The default employee cannot be paused or archived (409 `last_default`).
* `PUT /{id}/voices/{language}` `{voice_code}` (a voice of the catalogue for that language; any other 422). One voice per language the employee speaks.
* `PUT /{id}/phone` `{phone_number_id | null}` the workspace line this employee answers (one employee per line; a line with none goes to the default employee).
* `GET /{id}/activity?limit&before` -> `{calls: [{id, direction, state, purpose, language, outcome, duration_s, created_at}]}` the calls this employee took (the default employee's include every call made before the feature).
* `POST /{id}/preview` `{language, text?}` -> `{spoken_text, audio: {mime: "audio/wav", base64}, engine, voice_id}`: its opening line (or `text`, or a sample) in ITS voice. A voice that cannot speak answers 503 `voice_unavailable`; another voice never stands in. 429 `rate_limited` over the day's sample cap.
* `POST /assign` `{kind: "task" | "job", id, employee_id | null}` (Owner/Admin): the employee that does a task or a job (null: the default). A task's calls are made by it, inside its own hours (outside them the call is held: outcome `unclear`, category `call_back_later`).
* A test call as an employee: `POST /v1/tenants/{t}/calls/place` takes the new field `employee_id` (the employee that makes it; none = the default).

## Employee shape
`{id, name, role_code, role_title, status, is_default, responsibilities: [id], extra_instructions, languages, auto_switch_language, allow_english_mixing, style, speed, opening_line: {lang: text}, autonomy: {may_answer_prices, may_book_visits, may_send_media, may_talk_dues, max_discount_percent, handover_owner_asked, handover_upset, handover_order_above_paise}, ask_first: [action], hours: {days: [1..7], from_minute, to_minute} | null, never_say: [word], phone_number_id, whatsapp_number_ref, persona_in: "my_assistant" | "employee", voices: {lang: voice_code}, calls_today, created_at, updated_at}`.
* `role_code`: receptionist, sales, follow_up, collections, support, appointments, custom. `responsibilities` ids: enquiry_reply, faq_answers, visit_booking, quote_chase, lead_qualify, follow_up, review_request, payment_reminder, appointment_reminder (an unknown id is stored but not spoken). `ask_first` actions: send_message, make_call, give_discount, promise_date, book_visit, share_price, send_media (the default for a new employee: the first four).
* **The default employee** (`persona_in: "my_assistant"`): `name` is the assistant's name; `voices` are the workspace's voices; only `name`, `role_code`, `role_title`, `responsibilities`, `extra_instructions` and `never_say` are edited here; any other field answers 409 `settings_elsewhere` (the screen sends the owner to My assistant). Its voice and persona cannot be set per employee.
* **Hours** are days 1 (Monday) to 7 (Sunday) and minutes of the day in India time, never wider than 10:00 to 21:00 (422/`hours_wider` otherwise): they only NARROW the legal calling window. Outside them an inbound line is answered by the default employee.

## Channels are optional (owner rule, 26 Nov 2026; migration 992780)
WhatsApp, the phone and website chat are CHANNELS: responsibilities `channel_phone` (answers phone calls), `channel_whatsapp` (replies on WhatsApp), `channel_web_chat` (replies to website chat) that an AI employee has only when the owner ticks them. A business that never uses WhatsApp never sees it.
* `GET /v1/tenants/{t}/channels` (any member; also `channels` inside `GET /employees`) -> `{phone, whatsapp, web_chat: boolean, whatsapp_connection: {status: none|pending|active|disabled, connected, next_step: connect_whatsapp | activate_whatsapp | null}}`. A channel is on ONLY when at least one employee that is `on` has it. While `whatsapp` is false the web hides the WhatsApp setup, template tasks and Needs-you items about WhatsApp.
* Every Employee has `channels: {phone, whatsapp, web_chat}` (its own responsibilities) and `needs: [{channel: "whatsapp", step: "connect_whatsapp" | "activate_whatsapp"}]` when it has WhatsApp but the number is not active: turning the responsibility on never fails, it answers with the next step. Turning it off (a PUT without `channel_whatsapp`, or pausing the employee) pauses the channel for it; history is kept.
* A NEW workspace's default employee answers the phone only (`channel_phone`). The workspaces that exist today keep what they have: their default employee has phone and WhatsApp.
* The WhatsApp auto-reply answers a message only when an employee that is on has `channel_whatsapp` (the default employee first); otherwise the message is left for the owner (nothing drafted, nothing sent). The reply is written by that employee (its block, name and never-say words).

## Closed refusals (`error.code`, also `error.reason`)
409 `employee_limit` (the plan's number of employees), `name_taken` (case-insensitive, among live employees), `last_default`, `settings_elsewhere`; 422 `hours_wider`, `validation_error` (shape or value); 403/404 as usual.

## What a call does with an employee (flag on)
* WhatsApp auto-replies: the default employee's block, its name and its never-say words (a reply with one of them is held for the owner).
* Inbound: the line's employee (Phase 3), else the default. Outbound: the placing task's or test call's employee, else the default. An employee that is not `on`, or is outside its hours, never answers: the default does. The call row is tagged (`ai_employee_id`).
* The prompt gets ONE block after the platform rules: who it is, what it does, what it may not do alone, the owner's own words (as the owner's wishes, never above the rules). A non-default employee brings its own name, voices (a language it has no voice for gets the catalogue default, never the workspace's choice), speed, style and opening line (the AI disclosure is still added when it is missing). Every employee ADDS its never-say words to the workspace's.
* Above every employee, in code: the money guard, consent, DND, the calling window, the lawyer-opinion rules, outbound caps, the wallet gate.

## The owner assistant (plan cards)
Employees are write tools of the assistant catalogue (`employees.create_employee`, `employees.update_employee`, `employees.set_status`, `employees.set_voice`, `employees.set_phone`): the assistant proposes a plan card, the owner confirms. New setting `assistant_voice`: "change your voice to Ramya" is a plan card (language and voice name found in the catalogue; saved through the screen's own `PUT /ai-assistant/voices {language, voice_id}`, Owner or Admin; Undo puts the old voice back); a voice that is not in the catalogue for that language is named, never guessed. It changes the workspace's voice (My assistant, the default employee), not another employee's.
