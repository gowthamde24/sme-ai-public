# Contract: the in-app KYC of the business's phone number (C1 -> C2), 14 Nov 2026

**What it is** (owner and Plivo support, 13-14 Nov): "reseller" at Plivo = one subaccount per business + that business's OWN compliance application. **ONE document is enough: GST certificate, Certificate of Incorporation, or Udyam** (080 / 022 numbers, service and transactional calls, no DLT, no A2P declaration). Customers need no Plivo account. The Owner fills a form and attaches one PDF/JPG/PNG; Stack19 creates the subaccount (its token goes straight into Secret Manager) and passes the document through to Plivo **in memory only: it is never stored or logged**; we keep only the application's id and Plivo's status words. **Nothing here rents a number** (that stays a separate step that needs the owner's OK).

**Switch:** `PLIVO_KYC_LIVE` is OFF on staging until the owner says so for the first real filing (Customer Zero's own document). Off, `POST` checks the form and the file end to end, reads Plivo's requirements (a free read), and answers `{state:"not_started", live:false}`: nothing is sent, no subaccount is made. Draw the screen exactly the same; show a small "Test mode: nothing is sent yet" line when `live` is false.

## Routes (`/v1/tenants/{tenant_id}/phone-kyc`)
* `GET /options` (Owner, Admin) -> `{live, documents:[{kind:"gst"|"coi"|"udyam", number_label, number_example, plivo_document}], max_file_bytes:5000000, file_types:["application/pdf","image/jpeg","image/png"], kept_note}`. Labels are yours per language; `kind` and the rules are mine.
* `GET` (any member) -> `{state, kyc_id, doc_kind, compliance_id, reason, created_at, updated_at, checked_at, live, can_choose_number}`. `state`: `not_started | preparing | submitted | accepted | rejected | suspended | expired | failed`. `reason` = Plivo's own sentence for a rejection or a refusal (Owner and Admin only). **The Owner's own read (second factor on) also refreshes a `submitted` application from Plivo** (at most every 30 s), so "Check status" is a plain GET. `can_choose_number` is true once accepted.
* `POST` (Owner + second factor) `{kyc_id (yours, uuid), doc_kind, legal_name (as on the document), registration_number, email, address_line1, address_line2?, city, state, postal_code (6 digits), document:{name, content_type, data_base64}}` -> the same body as `GET`. A retry with the same `kyc_id` files once.
  * `registration_number` by kind (checked in shape, uppercased for you): GSTIN `29ABCDE1234F1Z5`; Udyam `UDYAM-KA-01-0123456`; CIN `U72200KA2020PTC123456` or LLPIN `AAB-1234`.
  * File: PDF, JPG or PNG, at most 5 MB, checked by its first bytes.
* Refusals: 413 `kyc_file_too_large`, 415 `kyc_file_type`, 422 `kyc_file_unreadable`, 422 `validation_error` (a field, e.g. a number of the wrong kind), **422 `kyc_not_accepted` with `reason` = Plivo's own words** (draw it; the Owner can fix and try again with a new `kyc_id`), 409 `kyc_document_not_offered` / `kyc_field_not_supported`, 502 `kyc_provider_unavailable` (try again, same `kyc_id`), 503 `kyc_unavailable` (no Plivo account configured). 403 for anyone but the Owner on `POST`; `mfa_required` without the second factor.
