# Plan: Lead Finder stores only what Google allows (decision of 12 Nov 2026; PLAN ONLY, nothing built)

Owner decision, route 1: from Google Places keep only the **place ID** (for good) and the **coordinates** (at most 30 days). Everything else (name, phone, website, address, rating, review count) is fetched fresh when a prospect is opened or called, used in memory, never saved. A business that answers and has a real conversation with us becomes a lead whose record is our own data. Source of the rule: Google's Places policies page, "Last updated 2026-11-09" (quoted in the addendum of ADR 0119). No real-data Lead Finder until this is done.

## What is stored today (migration `20261226090000_lf_prospects.sql`, table `public.prospects`)
Copied from Google: `name`, `name_key`, `category`, `area` (the address), `phone`, `phone_key`, `website`, `rating`, `review_count`; kept 30 days by `prospect_places_expire` (scheduler, every six hours). `place_id` (for good), `lat`/`lng` and `fetched_at` are allowed. Ours: `fit`, `fit_reason`, `stock_match`, `status`, `decision_reason`, `owner_approved`, `suppressed`, `ask_after`, `last_contacted_at`, `call_id`, `qualification`, `lead_id`.

## The change
1. **Data.** A migration makes the Google-copied columns nullable and, for `source = 'google_maps'`, never written: `prospect_*` functions take them for display only. The existing Google rows (Owner Test workspace only; no real data exists) are blanked by the same migration. `prospect_places_expire` then blanks the coordinates (not the other fields) after 30 days. The fixture source (synthetic businesses) keeps storing everything.
2. **Search.** The search still asks Places (`searchText`) and scores each result in memory (category, rating, distance are inputs). It saves the place ID, the coordinates, our decision and the score. The search response to the screen carries the live details once, with the "Google Maps" attribution.
3. **Opening a prospect / the list.** The API fetches Place Details by place ID (field mask: name, phone, website, address; only what that screen shows), holds the answer for the request, never writes it. A list page of N prospects = one batch of N fetches (each billed by Google; the per-fetch price to be read from Google's price list before this is built, and a short in-memory cache of at most a few minutes is NOT allowed to become storage). If Google is unreachable the screen says the details are unavailable.
4. **Calling.** Before a call the dialer fetches the number by place ID (in memory, into the call plan, as the outbound goal plan already keeps goals in memory). Nothing from Places is written to the call row except what the call itself produced.
5. **After the call.** If the business answered and talked with us, the lead is created from the call (what they said: their name as they gave it, what they want) and the number they answered on becomes the lead's contact. This is the point the lawyer is asked to confirm (question 8): the number first came from Google. Fallback if the answer is no: the assistant asks the person for their own contact detail in the call and the lead carries only that.
6. **Do not call.** A business that says "do not call" is suppressed by its **suppression key** (the HMAC of the number, the existing `SUPPRESSION_HMAC_KEY` design), not by a stored number; the key is ours (derived from a call that happened).
7. **Derived content.** `fit` and `fit_reason` are derived from Google fields (rating, category). Google's page forbids creating content "based on Google Maps Content" in some cases (its examples are tracing imagery and point-in-polygon on coordinates); a fit score is not one of its examples, but it is on the lawyer's list. If the answer is no, the score is recomputed at open time and not stored.
8. **Web (C2).** The prospect list and the practice-call screen read name and phone from the live fields of the API response; they must not cache them (no local or session storage, no server-side cache of the page).
9. **Tests.** pgTAP: the Google-copied columns stay null for `google_maps` rows; the coordinates expire after 30 days; the fixture source is unchanged. API: a search persists only place ID, coordinates, decision, score; Place Details fake returns the live fields; nothing but the allowed columns is written (a test that lists the columns written). Web: the screen shows the live fields, and nothing is stored.
10. **Docs.** ADR 0119 (done in its addendum), `docs/contracts/lead-finder.md`, `docs/data-inventory.md`, the sub-processor note (Google Maps Platform), the attribution rule.
