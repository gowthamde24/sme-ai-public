# ADR 0087: the education core: an institution, programmes, the minimal student record, admissions without companies, fees on the shared ledger, announcements and reports

Status: **ACCEPTED and BUILT (job CR, night run C1, 2026-10-15), for the owner's review.** Builds on ADR 0084 (parts 1, 2, 3 and 5: packs and modules, custom fields, reminder campaigns, children's data), ADR 0086 (the shared ledger and the campaign engine this uses), ADR 0014 (erasure), ADR 0015 (the real-data gate) and the blueprint's section 5.3. **The real-data gate stays closed for every education workspace until the DPDP children's review is filed** (decision 13).

## Context

A school, a college or a coaching centre lives on three things: the **admission enquiry** (a parent writes on WhatsApp, visits, applies, joins), the **fee** (instalments, a concession, a receipt, a reminder, a call), and **messages to parents** (an event, a holiday, an exam). Everything about a child is the most sensitive data the product could hold, so the record is the minimum ADR 0084 part 5 allows, and the rest of the system is built so that it cannot reach it.

## Decisions

### Structure

1. **The modules `programmes` and `fees` own tables** (they were `future`). The education pack already lists both; a business on any other pack cannot create a row (SM701). Every table has the module guard, row-level security (a client can only read), forced RLS, audit and, where a person can be named, a PII class.
2. **The generic enquiry path.** A lead that holds a contact needs a company (job T003), and a school has no companies module. `app.contact_lead(tenant, contact)` gets the contact's newest live lead or makes one, creating, when the contact has no company, the same kind of **generic placeholder company** that WhatsApp ingestion has made since job BR (`Enquiry <6 characters>`, `created_via = 'agent'`; job CK's one exemption of the companies guard is widened by exactly this name). The placeholder holds no personal data. Job CP's `app.campaign_lead_for` now delegates to it, which **closes CP's open item**: a guardian can be sent a campaign.
3. **The institution profile** (`institution_profiles`): the type (school, junior college, degree college, university, coaching, training), the month the academic year starts, up to six terms, the languages parents use, and the **retention rule** (decision 6). **Programmes** (`programmes`: a class set or a course, with what it is divided into: sections, years, semesters, batches or nothing) and **groups** (`programme_groups`: A, Year 2, Batch 6 am with a timing).

### The child

4. **The student record is the minimum, and the minimum is enforced where the data lives.** `students`: the adult we write to (`contact_id`: the guardian of a child, or the adult student themself), `child_label` (a first name or initials, up to 30 characters, **no digits, no e-mail address, no phone number**), the placement (programme, optional group), an optional school `admission_no`, a status (`active` or `left` with the day), the join day, and `erased_at`. **There is no column for** a date of birth, photograph, address, Aadhaar or any identity number, health, religion, caste, income, marks, attendance, behaviour, a note, a phone number, an e-mail address or a handle; a pgTAP check (`104` D38) fails if one is ever added, and every API request model forbids an unknown field (16 extra fields are tried by name in `tests/test_cr_routes.py`). A child is never a contact. An **adult student is the contact** (no label); they may also have a separate guardian contact.
5. **Child fields reach nothing but the people who run the school.** `child_label` and `admission_no` are PII columns (audited by name only, never by value). They are returned only by the education routes, to **Owner, Admin and Sales** (a Viewer sees no child: RLS and the functions both refuse). No model, log line, metric, error message, search index or default export can reach them, and this is **tested, not hoped**: (a) `tests/test_cr_child_fields.py` fails if any module outside the education package, the three named doors (the students file of the spreadsheet import, the privacy housekeeping, the wiring) and a comment, names a child column or the education repository, and if an agent, assistant, outbox, follow-up, scheduler, notification, voice or export module imports the education package; (b) `tests/integration/test_cr_education.py` plants a canary label in a school, drives the assistant (eleven questions, including asking for the canary and the children by name), the exports, the search, the reports and the error paths, **records every HTTP request the API makes to the data layer and every answer**, and proves the canary is in none of them, in no log line and in **exactly one table of the whole database** (`public.students`); (c) the message text of a fee reminder and an announcement has no merge field for a child (`name` is the guardian), and the preview and message tests assert no child's name is in a sample.
6. **Erasure cascades and retention exists.** Erasing a guardian (the existing contact scope) marks the contact; a trigger on that mark erases their students in the same transaction (label tombstoned, admission number removed, the row marked erased and kept, because money records point at it). The Owner, with a second factor, can erase **one child** on request (`student_erase`). The tenant-wide scope and the identifier sweep read the same registry rows (tests 39 and 104). **Retention:** `institution_profiles.student_retention_months` (the Owner's, second factor; null = none) erases a student who **left** that many months ago; the scheduler's housekeeping runs it (`scheduler_students_retention`), counts only. The number is the lawyer's.
7. **The rows change only through their functions.** A privileged session cannot edit a programme, a student, an enquiry or a fee plan either: a door (`app.edu_edit`) is opened around the function's own UPDATE; the erasure and the retention clean-up pass through their own door. Nothing here is ever deleted (a programme is made inactive, a student is marked left, an enquiry is lost).

### Admissions

8. **Admissions need no companies module.** `admissions` (the lead, the guardian, the programme asked about, the **source** WhatsApp, walk-in, website, advertisement, referral, other, the **stage** and, when lost, a closed reason) and `admission_events` (every stage reached, never changed). Stages: enquiry → campus visit → application → admitted | lost. Forward steps only (one may skip); lost from any open stage with a reason; a lost enquiry is re-opened by the Owner or an Admin; **admitted only through `admission_admit`**, which in ONE transaction makes the student (the guardian is the enquiry's contact; an adult student is that contact) and, with a plan, assigns it. A refusal anywhere undoes the whole admission (tested: a plan for another programme leaves no student and an enquiry still at its stage). **A campus visit is the existing visit** (job AS) of the guardian's lead; booking one moves an enquiry still at `enquiry` to `campus_visit` (a trigger; forward only). **A child who has not been admitted is not stored at all.** An enquiry can be started with a new guardian's name and WhatsApp number in one call (the contact gets its suppression keys like every contact).

### Fees on the shared ledger

9. **A student has their own ledger account** (`ledger_accounts.student_id`: one of company, contact or student). A **fee plan** (`fee_plans` + `fee_plan_lines`: a programme, optionally one group, an academic year, heads tuition/admission/transport/hostel/exam/books/other, instalments with due dates and amounts, an optional **late-fee rule**) cannot change once a student has been assigned it (SM813; make a new plan). **Assigning** a plan writes its instalments as charges on the student's account with ids derived from the assignment and the line (a retry writes nothing twice); one live plan per student per year (SM814); voiding an assignment voids its charges. The entry is dated the day of the assignment, or the due date if that is earlier. Every amount is a plan line or an entry a person made.
10. **Payments** (Owner, Admin, Sales) get the business's next receipt number (`R/2026-27/000001`, the one series of ADR 0086; a voided receipt keeps its number). Credits go to the **oldest claim first**, per student: a parent's payment for one child never lands on another's account. **Concessions** (Owner/Admin, second factor, a reason code: scholarship, sibling, staff ward, hardship, merit, other) are an amount or a percentage of what was charged to the student, **computed by the database** and never above what was charged. **Adjustments** (correction up with a due date, or down) are the same people with the same factor; `late_fee` is not a reason a person may give.
11. **Late fees are a rule the database computes and a person posts.** `fixed` = one amount per instalment still open after the grace days; `per_day` = days late times the amount, never above the cap. `fee_late_fees_preview` shows what the rule gives today (anyone who reads money); `fee_late_fees_post` (Owner/Admin, second factor) writes each as a debit adjustment against its instalment, with a derived id (once for `fixed`, once per day for `per_day`, minus what was already posted), so posting twice adds nothing twice. **Nothing is added to a parent's account by looking at it or by the clock.**
12. **Receipts and statements.** `fee_receipt_get` returns a receipt as data (number, date, amount, method, who paid, for whom, what it was set against, the balance after); `fee_statement` the ledger of one student with the age buckets (since the entry and past the due date); `fee_guardian_statement` what one guardian owes across their children. Printing is the web's.
13. **A reminder to a guardian of several children is ONE message with the total.** A campaign list built from the ledger with `party: "student"` groups the accounts of a guardian's students into one recipient (`ledger_scope = 'guardian'`: the total of the claims due in the range, the earliest date, no single reference when there are several). The campaign functions that read "what is still owed" (the runner, the call step, the results) use one helper (`app.recipient_outstanding`). The functions replaced for this are **full copies** pinned by `tests/test_migration_copies_cr.py` to differ by exactly the named lines. The message never names a child (the merge field `{{name}}` is the guardian; the reference is `Tuition 1`). *Known limit, inherited from job CP:* the check before a message compares the guardian's whole balance with the message's total, so a part payment inside the range does not shrink the message; only full payment or a fall below the total skips it.

### Announcements, import, reports

14. **Announcements are campaigns** of kind `event`, `exam` or `holiday` (service messages only; promotions stay out) with an audience chosen from the students: a section/batch, a programme or everyone (`campaign_recipients_from_students`), **one message per guardian however many children**, a guardian with no number or an erased contact skipped and counted. The Owner approves the whole campaign once, as always. A money kind cannot be filled from the students.
15. **The students file** is a third kind of the shared spreadsheet import (5,000 rows, in parts of 500): the parent's name and number, the class or course, the section, the first name or initials, the admission number, the joining day. A class or section the school does not have is reported (`needs_review`), never invented. A guardian is found by phone number or made, **and gets their suppression keys**; a refused row leaves nothing behind (no contact either); the preview names guardians, never a child; the job stores counts and line numbers.
16. **Reports** (`education_report`, in the one shape every pack's reports use: figures, typed tables, series by day): `education.collection` (collection by programme and by class/section: **due** is what has fallen due, **pending** what of it is still open, **collection %** is (due − pending)/due, so a concession counts as settled; collected and conceded in the window; by day), `education.today` (today's receipts by method), `education.pending` (the word is **pending**, never "defaulter"; also `fee_pending_list`, paged, largest first) and `education.funnel` (enquiries, campus visits, applications, admitted, lost, conversion, by source, by programme, by lost reason; it counts what was **reached**, so a lost enquiry still counts as an enquiry). All figures are sums of entries.

### The gate

17. **The real-data gate for a workspace that keeps student records (module `programmes`) opens only with a review reference that begins `dpdp-children:`** (SM816, `app.operator_open_real_data_gate`); the other businesses open with the ordinary reference as before. Until then every contact is synthetic (`+00` numbers).

## Not decided here, not built, and why

* **The web screens** (students and guardians import, fee plans, ledger, receipts, collection dashboard, admissions board, announcements): job CS, against the committed contract `packages/contracts/education.*`.
* **Parental consent for a child (DPDP section 9)**: who verifies it (the school or us), whether a school gets the conditional relief, the retention period, the processing agreement, the notice in each language: **the lawyer** (checklist). Nothing about a child goes to a model or a voice provider under this design.
* **Online payment links, attendance, marks, timetable, LMS, transport GPS, hostel, student photos or ID cards**: out of scope (the product says: "We focus on admissions, fees and messages to parents").
* **A per-child message**: a reminder for one child of a guardian with several is not offered; the guardian gets the total. A parent who wants it split would need the child's label in the message, which this design refuses.
* **An export of the students**: none exists (the child fields are off every default export by having no export); if one is ever wanted it needs the Owner, a second factor and an explicit field list.
* **Reviewers and native readers**: the education pack stays `preview` until a real school or college has reviewed it; te, hi, kn and ta words wait for a native reader (the students file headers in those languages are a first draft).

## Consequences

* Four migrations (`20261125090000` to `20261125090300`), all new files; full copies of earlier functions are listed in `tests/test_migration_copies_cr.py`. SQLSTATEs SM811 to SM816. New pgTAP files 104 to 107; registry rows, role-matrix rows and a canary for the erasure planter.
* Job CP's `campaign_lead_for` no longer returns null for a contact with no company (the pgTAP expectations of 101 changed with it, on purpose).
