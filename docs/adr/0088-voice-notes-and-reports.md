# ADR 0088: voice notes (ADR 0084 part 4, V1 and V2) and the one reports API

Status: **ACCEPTED and BUILT (job CV, night run C1, 2026-10-15), for the owner's review.** Builds on ADR 0084 part 4 (voice settings, the fixed AI-disclosure line, voice notes before calls), ADR 0071 (speech behind interfaces; the fair-use ledger), ADR 0086 (campaigns and their approval hash), ADR 0087 (the education pack) and the blueprint's section 5. **No paid service, no key, no provider is switched on**: the only text-to-speech engine that can run is the fake one, in development. **V3 (automated outbound calls) and V4 (voice cloning) are NOT built** and stay blocked on the lawyer's and the telephony provider's answers (ADR 0084 part 4).

## Context

A school's fee reminder or an event notice is read by a parent who often prefers to listen, in their own language. ADR 0084 recommended that the first spoken use be a **WhatsApp audio message** made by text-to-speech, needing no telephony, with a fixed spoken disclosure that a machine made it. Separately, every pack needs its numbers: the construction pack's quotes, revenue, trips and dues; the school's collection and funnel; the existing weekly summary. One shape for all of them lets one web screen draw any.

## Decisions

### Voice notes

1. **The module `voice_notes` owns tables** (it was `future`; the education pack lists it, so education is the first pack that has it on). Default deny: a business on any other pack gets SM701 from every function. Voice is **off by default** for a business that has the module (`voice_settings.enabled`).
2. **Settings per business** (`voice_settings`: the switch, a speed between 0.75 and 1.25, the calling hours inside 9 to 21 that the call list also respects; recording is **always false** and there is no column for it). The Owner sets them, **with a second factor**; the Owner and Admin read them. A **voice for each language** (`voice_choices`) is chosen from the operator's closed list (`voice.voices`, no client role can read or write the schema). Only **synthetic machine voices** exist (`fake-en`, `fake-te`, `fake-hi`, `fake-kn`, `fake-ta`); a real voice enters the list only with a native speaker's name and date (`reviewed_by`, `reviewed_at`; a check constraint refuses an unreviewed non-synthetic voice). **No voice is cloned and none imitates a person.**
3. **The spoken disclosure is fixed and cannot be removed.** `voice.disclosures` holds one wording per language with exactly one slot, the business's name ("This is an automated voice message from the AI assistant of {{business}}."). The five first wordings are **drafts** until a native speaker has marked them reviewed; a **draft may be spoken only while the real-data gate is closed** (synthetic data), a reviewed one anywhere. After the gate opens a language with no reviewed wording **cannot** produce a voice note: the campaign goes as text. The disclosure is spoken, not added to the stored text, so the text the Owner approved and the text a customer reads are the same.
4. **A voice note is an addition, never a replacement.** A campaign has a voice switch (`campaign_voice`, off by default, Owner/Admin/Sales like any edit of the campaign). When it is on, the worker sends the approved **text** to the channel as before, and **also** an audio clip of the disclosure followed by the same words. If anything is missing (the module, either switch, the voice, the wording, the allowance, the length over 600 characters, the provider, a refused record) the message goes as text alone. The text is never held back for the audio and a repeat of a lapsed claim counts the audio once.
5. **The switch is part of what the Owner approves.** `app.campaign_content_sha` (job CP's approval hash) is replaced by a full copy that appends the voice signature (the voice, the wording's version and status) **only while the switch is on**, so every approval made before this job still verifies. Turning the switch on or off sends an approved campaign back to draft (the same rule as editing its text) and the old preview hash can no longer be approved. A copy-pin test (`tests/test_migration_copies_cv.py`) fails if the function is redefined after this migration or differs from CP's by anything but those lines.
6. **The audio is made at send time and never stored.** The worker asks the database (`scheduler_voice_plan`: the registered scheduler account only) whether the claimed message is a voice note and what to say; synthesises through the `TextToSpeech` interface (domain code never names a provider); **records the seconds first** (`scheduler_voice_record`, one row per message: a repeat counts once); hands the clip to the adapter in memory. No table holds audio, a transcript or the spoken words (a test reads the information schema). The clip is excluded from every `repr` and log line; only the class of an error is logged.
7. **Fair use is counted in seconds, never in rupees or tokens** (`voice_note_usage`). Each plan has a daily and a monthly allowance in `plan_voice_allowances` (placeholders: free trial 120 and 1,200 s; starter 300 and 6,000; growth 900 and 18,000; business 2,400 and 48,000 **for the owner to set**); no plan row means zero (fail closed). The state is **ok** under 80 percent, **warn** from 80, **paused** from 100 of either window; paused removes the audio and keeps the text.
8. **A preview** (Owner, Admin) speaks the disclosure line only, at most 20 seconds, at most ten an hour, under the same ledger, with the caller's own id so a retry counts once. The API makes the preview (it is the only audio the API ever returns); the worker makes real notes.
9. **WhatsApp specifics (honest degrade).** Meta allows an audio message only inside the customer's 24-hour window and only in some formats (ogg opus, mp3, aac, mp4, amr; **a WAV is not accepted**). The adapter therefore uploads and sends the audio **after** the text, **only inside the window**, **only in an accepted format**, best effort: a refusal is logged by its code, never changes the outcome of the message and is never retried (a retried text would be a duplicate). Outside the window the approved template carries the text and no audio goes. A real provider adapter must return an accepted format (conversion is a later step; nothing here converts audio). The local fake channel keeps the clip for tests.
10. **Speed and voice are stored and passed with the plan**, but the existing `TextToSpeech` interface takes only text and language; applying the chosen voice and speed to a real provider is part of wiring that provider (each adapter picks its own speaker from the owner's settings today). The fake provider ignores both.

### Reports

11. **One shape for every pack's report.** `GET /reports/catalogue` and `GET /reports/run/{code}?from=&to=` return `{report, as_of, from, to, kpis: [{key, value, unit}], tables: [{key, columns: [{key, type}], rows}], series: [{key, points: [{day, value}]}]}` with units paise, count, percent and seconds. The catalogue lists only the reports whose modules are **all** on for the business and whose role list includes the caller (a Viewer is offered none); running one that is off is SM701. The set is a closed list in the database (`app.report_definitions`): `weekly.summary` (wraps the existing weekly report, Owner and Admin), `construction.quotes | revenue_by_product | trips_per_day | dues_ageing | top_customers`, `education.collection | today | pending | funnel` (job CR). No pack name is read: a report is available because its modules are on.
12. **Every figure is a sum or a count of entries a person made**; a model never computes or reads one. "Won" in the construction quotes report means an approved quote that became an order. Dues ageing and top customers use the shared ledger's allocation rule, so they equal the statement a customer sees. The word is "pending", never "defaulter".

## SQLSTATEs
22023 invalid, 42501 not allowed, 23503 not found, SM701 module off, SM306 second factor, SM801 campaign finished (a finished campaign's voice cannot change).

## Not done, and why
* **Automated calls (V3) and voice cloning (V4):** blocked on the lawyer's and provider's answers and a paid telephony approval (ADR 0084 part 4).
* **A real text-to-speech provider for notes:** the Sarvam, Bhashini, Google and AI4Bharat adapters exist behind the interface (job BD) and are off; switching one on (and converting its audio to an accepted format) is the owner's decision once a key and a spend cap exist. The reviewed voice list and the five reviewed disclosure wordings wait for native speakers.
* **The allowance numbers** are placeholders for the owner.
* **Web screens** (the voice switch, the voice and disclosure pickers, the meter, the reports page) are the web lane's, built against `packages/contracts/voice_notes.*` and `reports.*`.

## Consequences
A business can offer parents a spoken copy of a reminder in their language at a cost that is counted in seconds and capped, with the machine's nature stated aloud, without a second approval path, a stored recording or a vendor lock-in. Reports for all packs are one component on the web.
