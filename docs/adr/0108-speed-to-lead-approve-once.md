# ADR 0108: speed to lead and "approve once" (job DR, night 4 Part A3)

Status: built on branch `api/dr-speed-approvals` (27 Oct 2026), stacked on `api/ds-spoken-style`. Source: the owner's decision that a person approves a PLAN, not each contact. Calls and WhatsApp stay fake (no telephony exists); the plan, the approvals, the timing, the caps, the outcomes, the hand-off and the reports are real.

## Decision
1. **One approval per plan.** A batch (an upload, a list, a source filter) is planned into `outreach_batches` + `outreach_items`; the plan (counts, skipped by reason, three masked samples, the window, estimated minutes and cost, the caps) is hashed (`plan_sha256`) over the batch settings and the ordered list of contacts to be reached. The Owner approves with a second factor and sends the hash; a different hash is `409 plan_changed`. Excluding people before approval changes the hash; after approval it stops their waiting steps. Staff (Owner, Admin, Sales) can build drafts; nobody else's click is needed per contact.
2. **Standing rules per lead source** (`outreach_rules`): "new leads from `meta_lead_ads`: call within 2 minutes in calling hours" approved once by the Owner with a second factor. The worker's scan (`scheduler_outreach_scan`) turns each new lead of that source into an item at arrival time; nothing is retroactive.
3. **What happens to one lead is code, not a model** (functions `app.outreach_*`): in hours, the first call at arrival + N minutes; after hours, WhatsApp now (if allowed and consented) and the call next morning at `night_call_minute`; no answer, busy or voicemail retry after `retry_gap_hours` inside hours up to `max_attempts`, then WhatsApp, then stop; a refusal, STOP or a wrong number stops the lead for good (STOP goes into the suppression list); only the workspace's own contacts, never a stranger; skipped (counted, with reasons) when there is no number, no consent for the channel, a suppression, an erasure, or a duplicate number inside the batch. A customer is called at most `max_calls_per_customer_per_week` (the call settings) times a week.
4. **Caps pause, they do not fail.** A day's call cap or a total-minutes cap pauses the batch or rule (`paused_reason`) and puts a "Calling is paused" item on Today for the Owner and Admin (kind `outreach_paused`). The day cap lifts itself next morning; the minutes cap waits for the Owner to raise it.
5. **Outcomes of the generic pack:** `already_bought` joins `interested`, `visit_booked`, `not_interested` (with a reason), `call_back_later` (with `callback_at`) and `wrong_number`; `outcome_details` holds `callback_at` and the pack's `captured` fields (budget, area, ...). Every outcome, the lead timeline and the totals carry them.
6. **Hot-lead hand-off:** a positive outcome assigns the lead to the Sales member with the fewest open hand-offs (else Admin, else Owner), writes a one-line summary, and reminds the assignee once after `handoff_reminder_minutes` (default 15). The reminder appears on Today (`hot_lead_waiting`) and rides the existing web-push. The hand-off table is readable by Owner and Admin directly; the assignee reads theirs through the function and Today.
7. **Reports:** per source and total, by day range: leads, median first-touch seconds, calls, connected rate, interested %, bookings, call cost (minutes x the policy's paise per minute), lead cost (from `lead_source_costs`, set by the Owner) and cost per booking.
8. **The dialer is an interface** (`app/outreach/runner.py`). The only implementation is the fake (`OUTREACH_DIALER=fake`, set by `deploy/staging.sh` for the worker): it answers by the last digit of the number. Without a dialer the worker leaves calls in the queue but still finds new leads and sends the reminders. WhatsApp steps are written by the database through `app.campaign_submit` (category `campaign_reminder`), so consent, quiet hours, the word check and the switches apply.

## Consequences
- Approving, resuming and saving a rule are "spend" requests: refused while the workspace is read-only (the billing gate).
- A second migration cannot change an approved plan's meaning: the hash is recomputed by the same function (`app.outreach_plan_sha`); changing it later would need a new version marker.
- When telephony exists, a real dialer replaces the fake behind the same interface and the same two door functions (`scheduler_outreach_run`, `outreach_item_result`); the database rules do not change.
- `lead_source_costs` is shared with part A2 (lead sources tag every lead with a source string, `excel_upload`, `indiamart`, `meta_lead_ads`, ...); either branch creates it with `create table if not exists`.
