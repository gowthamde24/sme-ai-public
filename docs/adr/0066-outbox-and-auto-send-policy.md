# ADR 0066: the outbox, the auto-send policy and call requests

Status: accepted (job AT, 2026-10-10). Builds on ADR 0013 (agents act in a person's name), ADR 0020 (suppression keys), ADR 0022 (follow-ups) and ADR 0065 (the adapter ports). Satisfies non-negotiable 3 ("no external communication without an explicit policy and approval path") for WhatsApp.

## Context
The owner decided (project decision "auto-send policy"): the agent sends WhatsApp **automatically except financial and sensitive content**, and **calls only after the owner approves**. Until now every outgoing word was a person's own act (follow-up drafts are approved, then the person sends and records it). The decision needs a place where "what may go by itself" is decided by rules, not by a model, and where everything that leaves is recorded. The text of the decision file (`decision-auto-send-policy.md`) was not in the repository; this ADR records the decision as given in the job brief.

## Decision
1. **Every outgoing message is a row** (`outbox_messages`): lead and contact, channel (`whatsapp` only), text, language, category, status (`queued | needs_approval | sent | blocked | failed`), a closed reason, the content flags found, who made it and who decided. Rows are never deleted; their state moves along a fixed machine (needs_approval -> queued | blocked; queued -> sent | failed | blocked); a sent, failed or blocked row is closed.
2. **The policy is deterministic and lives in the database** (`app.outbox_hard_block`, `app.outbox_decide_content`), not in a prompt. First match wins:
   - *blocked*: kill switch off (`switch_off`), contact erased, **customer stopped** (contact suppressed, WhatsApp consent withdrawn, or the number's suppression key is suppressed under any record), no phone, **no suppression key on record** (ADR 0020: missing data never means "not suppressed"), no opt-in and no contact history, the per-customer **daily limit** (default 3, counted over the India day).
   - *needs approval* (the default): money (a rupee sign, rupee words, price, discount, GST, advance, payment, refund, credit, UPI, bank... in English, Telugu, Hindi, Kannada and Tamil), a commitment (promise, guarantee, warranty...), a complaint (in the text, or in the customer's latest message), a flag from the model (`model_flagged`, `uncertain`), an uncategorised message, a "reply" with no inbound WhatsApp message in the last 24 hours, or a business that has not switched automatic sending on.
   - *queued (automatic)*: a reply within 24 hours of the customer's message, catalogue / hours / location, visit booking / confirmation / reminder, a follow-up without prices, an order status without amounts. **Quiet hours 21:00 to 09:00 India time**: queued with `scheduled_for` the next 09:00.
   A model can only make a message wait (its flags raise the bar); nothing a model says lowers it.
3. **Defaults are closed.** The platform flag `outbox_enabled` (the global kill switch) is OFF; a business's automatic sending is OFF (`outbox_settings`, Owner with a second factor). The hard blocks are checked again at approval and again at dispatch; while the kill switch is off nothing is claimed and the queue waits.
4. **An approval is a human decision.** Owner or Admin, with a second factor, may approve text the policy held back (including money); Owner or Admin may edit (the first-message disclosure line stays) or reject. A STOP (`suppress_contact`) or an erasure blocks every message of that contact that has not gone, at once (a trigger on `contacts`).
5. **The first message to a customer carries the AI-assistant disclosure line in their language** (en, te, hi, kn, ta). A first message that is blocked does not use up "first".
6. **Channel adapter interface, local fake only** (`app/outbox/adapter.py`). A message leaves only through an adapter; the only one is `LocalFakeAdapter`, which opens no connection. `OUTBOX_ADAPTER` accepts only `fake`. A real WhatsApp adapter is a later job that needs the owner's written approval (ADR 0017) and must use the message id as its idempotency key (a delivery whose result could not be recorded is offered again after two minutes).
7. **Calls**: `call_requests` (lead, reason, script draft, language). Anyone who may message may ask; **only the Owner, with a second factor, approves or rejects**; the approval is recorded (who, when, note) and audited. Nothing dials. A customer who asked us to stop cannot be called (SM502).
8. **Owner-facing reads**: the outbox log (Owner, Admin, Sales; a Viewer reads none, a message can hold a price), the needs-approval queue and Today (`outbox_approval` for Owner and Admin, `call_approval` for the Owner).

## Consequences
* New SQLSTATEs: SM501 (not in a state that allows this), SM502 (the customer asked us to stop). Everything else reuses 42501, 22023, 23503, 23505, SM306.
* The audit trail records that a message changed (columns named, never the text).
* Erasure: the texts are registered for the tenant scope, the sweep, and the contact and company scopes (`app.erase_contact` and `app.erase_company` are replaced as full copies, pinned by `test_migration_copies.py`): erasing a person tombstones the messages written to them and the call requests about them.
* Dispatch has no scheduler yet. Owner and Admin dispatch when they submit or approve and through `POST /outbox/dispatch`; a worker needs a dedicated principal (ADR 0013 option B) before the first scheduled agent.
* Sent outbox messages do not yet write a `lead_touches` row, so the follow-up cadence does not know about them: the follow-up desk integration is the next step.
* Today and the Main agent: Today's waiting list gains `outbox_approval` (Owner, Admin) and `call_approval` (Owner); the Main agent may cite them as sources (`outbox_message`, `call_request`; labels never carry the text). A web build that does not know the two kinds rejects Today while one waits: the web ships with this API.
* Word lists err on the side of asking a person (Indic words are matched as substrings). A message that needs the words changed is an edit, not a code change; the lists are one function (`app.outbox_content_flags`) and its pgTAP table.
