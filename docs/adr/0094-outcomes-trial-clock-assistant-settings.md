# ADR 0094: Outcomes as data, the 14-day trial counted from WhatsApp, the AI assistant's settings, and fixed templates for children's results

Status: **built and tested locally (job DM, C1 part, 25 Oct 2026); nothing deployed; no account opened; sending stays fake.** Owner decisions of 25 Oct 2026 (evening) and C2's questions (`~/Desktop/stack19-jobs/c2-questions.md`). Builds on ADR 0092 (plans and billing), ADR 0084 (packs, campaigns, voice, children's data) and ADR 0093 (real voice notes groundwork).

## 1. Outcomes are data
After each call and each WhatsApp follow-up conversation the AI records an **outcome**: `positive | negative | unclear`, a category (`interested`, `visit_booked`, `order_likely` | `not_interested`, `wrong_number`, `too_costly` | `call_back_later`) and a one-line reason. The owner (or an admin) can correct it; what the AI said is kept. Table `public.outcomes` (migration 20261129090000), written only by SECURITY DEFINER functions: `outcome_record` (the registered channel account, or an Owner, Admin or Sales person), `outcome_correct`, and the reads `outcomes_list`, `outcomes_for_lead`, `outcomes_totals` (India days, zero-filled). One outcome per `(kind, source)`; **one outcome per customer per conversation (24 hours)** when the AI records: a later reply can only turn the AI's unclear or positive word into a negative one, and a person's outcome or correction is never overwritten. The AI records from the **customer's reply** with a cautious word list in English, Hindi, Telugu and Kannada (a first draft for native review; a clear signal only; the reason is a fixed sentence, never the customer's words), after the webhook stored the reply and only if we wrote to that customer in the last 14 days. "STOP" is a negative `not_interested` (the suppression is the existing rule). **Calls:** telephony is not built; the call pipeline will record with `kind: "call"`. **No child data:** no reference to a student; in an education workspace the reason is never stored; outcomes of fee reminders or marks messages are not AI outcomes (those messages are templates). Contract: `docs/contracts/outcomes.md`.

## 2. The trial is 14 days from the day WhatsApp is connected
Plans catalogue **version 2**: `trial.days = 14`, `trial.starts_on = "whatsapp_connected"`, `trial.setup_days_without_whatsapp = 30` (version 1 is untouched: a synced version is never edited). `integrations.whatsapp_accounts.connected_at` is set once, the first time a number is active, and never moved. A plan chosen **before** WhatsApp is connected is **`trial_pending`** (owner decision): `trial_ends_at` is null, the answer says `trial_waiting_for: "whatsapp"`, **nothing is charged**, the workspace can set up everything (`access: full`), but **sending, AI calls and campaign sends stay off** until WhatsApp is connected (the outbox claim hands out nothing, the call start is refused; messages stay queued and nothing is lost; behind the existing `billing_enforced` switch). Connecting turns it into `trialing` with `trial_ends_at = connected day + 14 days`. Waiting never makes a workspace read-only; if WhatsApp is still not connected 30, 60 and 90 days after the plan was chosen, the Owners are sent a reminder e-mail (fake sender; the same fake sender as ADR 0092). The provider is told the first-charge day by the worker once it is known (`BillingProvider.set_trial_end`; `scheduler_billing_trial_clocks_due`). A workspace with **no** subscription lapses 14 days after WhatsApp was connected, or 30 setup days after it was made if it never connected. Terms: staging sets `TERMS_VERSION` and `PRIVACY_VERSION` to `2026-10-25`.

## 3. The AI assistant's settings (Settings > AI assistant)
`public.assistant_settings` (one row per workspace, made by the first save; until then the API shows the **defaults of the business type**, data in `app/assistant_settings/data/assistant.json`: name, voice persona, languages, way of talking, speed, what it may do on calls, the highest discount, when it hands over, calling days and hours). `voice.voice_personas` maps the four personas (female warm, female clear, male calm, male lively) to a voice per language (fake voices today; a real voice is added by the operator after a native speaker listened). The **Owner** saves with a second factor; Owner and Admin read. A save also hands the speed, the calling hours and the persona's voices to the business's **voice notes** (job CV) when it has them: one setting, used by calls and voice replies. **"AI assistant" is always said:** the introduction is composed from fixed wording per language (`"{name}, the AI assistant of {business}"`), is not a stored field, and the name carries no markup. **The school rule (owner, confirmed 25 Oct 2026):** an education workspace's assistant **never discusses dues, fees or marks on calls or chats, offers no discount and has no order limit**; those go only as fixed template messages from the school's records. The save refuses any setting that allows it (database and API), and the answer lists the `locked` fields. "Call me now for a test" is a **simulation** until telephony exists (no call, nothing sent or stored).

## 4. Marks and progress messages name the child: fixed templates, never the AI
`app/education/progress.py`: two fixed templates (`marks`, `report_ready`) in four languages (draft wording for native review), filled by plain replacement from values a person at the school supplied from the school's records (the child's first name, subject, marks, maximum, term). The module imports nothing that talks to a model, a speech engine or the network; nothing on the model side (agents, assistant, voice, follow-ups, outbox, WhatsApp, scheduler, campaigns) imports it; the assistant has no tool that reaches it; no campaign kind carries marks (tests prove each). **Not built, and the owner's decision:** where the values come from at send time and whether the platform may ever *store* children's results (DPDP: verifiable parental consent). Today no table holds marks (a pgTAP test scans every column). The education pack has no lead finder and no research (a test and the module guard).
