# ADR 0067: store visits, the opening hours, and what Today needs

Status: accepted (job AS, 2026-10-11). Builds on ADR 0066 (the outbox and the send policy: its clock seam and its policy decide every message a visit makes), ADR 0021 (orders) and ADR 0013 (agents act in a person's name).

## Context
Most of Customer Zero's customers do not pay in advance. They visit the shop (or call) after a visit has been booked. Until now the product had quotes, orders and follow-ups but no place for the thing that actually happens first: "Priya is coming on Saturday at 4". Today had a section "At the store today" built behind data that did not exist. The owner asked for: store hours and slots, visits with a small set of states and an outcome, an order from a visit, Agent OWL proposing a visit, a confirmation and a reminder through the outbox, "offer a store visit" on a call request, a Today list, and everything under the usual rules (RLS, roles, audit, erasure, India time, a pinnable clock).

## Decision
1. **Opening hours** (`store_settings`, one row per business, created by the first save; a business with no row has defaults): the whole week (Monday to Sunday, each day closed or `open`/`close` as `HH:MM`, close up to `24:00`), single closed dates, a slot length (15, 20, 30, 45, 60, 90 or 120 minutes) and how many visits at once (1 to 50). Defaults: every day 10:00 to 20:30, 30-minute slots, 2 at once. Owner or Admin edit; every member reads; no second factor (the hours hold no money). Saving the hours changes no visit already booked.
2. **A slot belongs to the India date it starts on.** All slot logic is `Asia/Kolkata`. A visit takes one slot; the last slot of a day that closes at `24:00` ends exactly at midnight and is still that day's; a slot at 00:30 India time is the next day's although its UTC date is the day before. The visit row stores `visit_date` (checked equal to the India date of `starts_at`), `starts_at` and `ends_at`.
3. **Booking** (`visit_book`): the date must not have passed (an earlier slot of today is allowed: a walk-in being written down), the shop must be open, the start must be one of the day's slots, and fewer than `max_parallel` active visits (booked, confirmed, arrived) may overlap it. The check holds a per-day advisory lock, so two bookings cannot both take the last place. The same customer cannot hold two live visits that start at the same moment. A customer whose record was erased cannot be booked. A retry with the same `visit_id` replays; the same id with other content is a conflict.
4. **States**: `booked -> confirmed -> arrived -> completed`, and `booked | confirmed -> cancelled | no_show`, `booked -> arrived` (a walk-in), `confirmed -> booked` only by moving the visit (the customer must confirm the new time). Arriving is only on the day of the visit; a no-show only once its time has come; an arrived visit can only be completed. The same step twice replays. A database guard trigger enforces the machine and which columns may change even for the owner of the database, and no visit is ever deleted or truncated.
5. **Outcome on completion**: `bought`, `will_return` or `not_interested`.
6. **An advance is optional and never required**: a flag plus an amount in paise (1 to 10 lakh rupees), off by default, and it never blocks a booking, a confirmation or a completion.
7. **An order from a visit reuses the existing order flow.** Completing a visit as `bought` with an `order_id` calls `create_order_from_quote` for an APPROVED quote of this customer (the one named, else the newest approved quote with no order yet). So an order here is the first state of the existing flow, `quote_approved`; there is no "draft order without a quote" (orders in this product always come from an approved quote, ADR 0021). It needs an Owner or Admin with a second factor, and it is all or nothing: if the order cannot be made (no approved quote, the quote already has an order, the order policy refuses) the visit stays `arrived`.
8. **Messages go through the outbox (ADR 0066) under its policy.** A confirmation (of a confirmed visit) or a reminder (of a confirmed visit that has not started) is a `visit`-category message from fixed templates in five languages (`app/visits/texts.py`), made by `visit_send_message`, which calls `outbox_submit`: it goes by itself outside quiet hours, waits for 09:00 inside them, is blocked for a customer who asked us to stop, and **waits for a person if it mentions money** (the template adds the advance sentence only when an advance was asked for, which the money word list catches). The message ids are stable (visit, kind, start, language, advance), so a retry replays and a visit that moved or changed its advance makes a new message. The visit links its confirmation and its reminder; the list says whether they really went out. **The Telugu, Hindi, Kannada and Tamil templates are machine drafts that no native reader has checked**; until `CHECKED_LANGUAGES` names a language, every message in it is submitted as `uncertain` and waits for a person. The outbox policy matches Indian-language words as substrings, so a template can trip it by accident: three did while this was written (a Tamil month name ends in the word for tax, the Tamil "confirmed" holds the word for a guarantee, a Kannada "if it changes" holds "rate"); the templates avoid those words and an integration test runs every template, language and many dates through the database's own word check.
9. **"Offer a store visit"** (`POST /call-requests/{id}/offer-visit`, Owner, second factor): one database function declines the call request with the fixed note `offer_store_visit` and submits the offer message to the outbox, in one transaction. The same call again replays (it finds its own message); a second offer for a decided request is refused. For a customer who asked us to stop the call is still declined and the message is blocked (`stopped`).
10. **Agent OWL can propose a visit** (tool `propose_visit`: lead, date, time). It books a visit with `source = assistant` and nothing else: no party size, no purpose, no advance (the person adds them), status `booked` (never confirmed), and leaves a card that opens the visit. The hours, slots and places are the database's. The tool is deliberately small: every tool is sent to the model with every question and job AO's budget is 3,400 estimated tokens a question; this tool costs 77 per call (+154 a question), so the ceiling in `test_assistant_tokens.py` moved from 3,400 to 3,450 and no more.
11. **Today**: `GET /visits/today` lists today's (India date) visits that are still to come or here now, in time order, with the exact fields the web asked for (`who`, `extra`, `topic`, and the three words `confirmed | reminder_sent | requested`, plus the true status `visit_status`). It is its own endpoint rather than a change to `GET /today`: Today already reads several things separately, and it keeps `public.today_summary` (a full-copy function) untouched.
12. **The weekly AI figure**: `GET /ai-usage/week` (Owner, Admin) is one percentage and a reset time. The week is Monday to Sunday India time; its allowance is an even share of the month's (monthly x 7 / the days of the billing month). It is a separate read so `GET /ai-usage` keeps its shape.
13. **The clock**: every visit function reads `app.visit_now()`, which is `app.outbox_now()` (the ADR 0066 seam: `now()` at run time, replaced by a test inside its own transaction). Nothing at run time can move it.

## Consequences
* New tables `store_settings` and `store_visits` (RLS forced, every member reads, nobody writes directly, audited without the free text, registered for erasure). `purpose`, `note` and `cancel_reason` are erased with the person or the company (`app.erase_contact` and `app.erase_company` are replaced as full copies, pinned by `test_migration_copies.py`) and swept. `assistant_save_reply` is copied once more so `store_visit` is a source and a draft the Main agent may save.
* New SQLSTATEs: SM511 (shop closed), SM512 (not a slot), SM513 (slot full), SM514 (date passed), SM515 (no approved quote to make the order from); SM501/SM502 are reused with the visit meaning. The API maps each to one fixed sentence.
* A visit with a lead that has no contact can be booked (a phone call, no name yet); no message can be made for it (the outbox blocks it: no contact).
* Reading is open to every member (Today shows who is coming to anyone who may read leads); the note is visible to a Viewer, as the lead's contact already is.
* There is no scheduler: reminders are made by a person (or by a later worker with its own principal, ADR 0013 option B). Customer replies ("yes, I will come", a STOP) are still recorded by a person.
* The seed (`make seed-demo`) now also books eight invented visits (today and tomorrow); the step the clock does not allow yet is left undone.
