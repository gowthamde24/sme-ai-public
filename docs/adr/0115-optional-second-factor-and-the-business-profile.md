# ADR 0115: the second factor is optional and asked once per session, delete and full export need the typed business name, the Owner edits the business profile (job DT, the owner's first real sign-in)

Status: built on branch `api/dt-first-run` (28 Oct 2026), from `main`. Source: the owner's words after the first real Google sign-in (fix-first-run.md): "We don't need authentication again, Gmail is enough", "once I set the business name I can't edit it, so the voice assistant says the wrong business name", and the 20:14 additions (two-factor optional for everyone, sensitive actions need a recent sign-in, profile pictures). Supersedes ADR 0016 where they differ. **Corrected the same day by the owner (28 Oct, evening): NO 10-minute window.** The first version of this ADR asked for a sign-in within 10 minutes before a sensitive action; the owner decided against any time window. The decisions 1 to 5 below are the corrected ones (the migration 20261211090300 replaces the first version's functions).

## Decision
1. **The second factor is optional for everybody** (Google, Facebook and e-mail alike): never asked at sign-up or at setup. A person who TURNED IT ON (a verified authenticator in `auth.mfa_factors`) enters the code **once per sign-in session** and is never asked again in that session: the session is then `aal2` and stays so until it ends, however long that is. A person who did not turn it on is never asked, whatever the age of the session. `app.require_aal2()` keeps its name (111 call sites) and means exactly that: if the person has an authenticator on and the token does not say `aal2`, refuse with SQLSTATE `SM306` and the sentence clients and tests know. There is **no time window of any kind**.
2. **The API asks the same single question** (`require_tenant_role(strong=True)`: Owner and Admin only, after the role is proven): on a session that is not `aal2` it asks the database whether the person has the authenticator on (`account_security_get`) and answers 403 `mfa_required` with `reason: "second_factor"` when they do; when the database cannot be asked the answer is "yes" (fail closed). A session that is `aal2` costs no extra call.
3. **The client-write trigger on `memberships` and `tenants`** asks the same one question (a security invoker; the check goes through `app.my_second_factor_on()`, the one definer helper a client role may run: it answers for the caller only, allow-listed in the catalog guard).
4. **`GET /v1/account/security`** says `second_factor: "optional"`, `second_factor_on`, `provider`, `session_level`. Nothing about times. Turning the authenticator on and off is Supabase's own call from the browser (enrol / unenrol); the Auth server itself demands `aal2` to remove a verified factor, which this ADR does not change. The demo logins keep their authenticator (so they are still asked for the code, once per session).
5. **Delete and full export need the business name typed, and tell the Owner afterwards** (this replaces the earlier idea of asking for a fresh sign-in for them). `POST /v1/tenants/{t}/data-exports` now needs `confirm_name` (the business name as written, surrounding spaces ignored; 422 `confirmation_mismatch` otherwise, and nothing is started); `POST /v1/tenants/{t}/deletion-requests` already needed `confirm_name` (checked by the database, SM615). The person's own account deletion keeps its typed e-mail address. After the action succeeded, a background task e-mails the Owner who did it (fixed words in `app/privacy/notice.py`: a full copy was requested / deletion was requested, with how to cancel / deletion was carried out), to the address in the sign-in token. The notice is sent only after success, can never undo or block the action (a failure to send is logged without the address), and a missing sender or address just means no notice. Only the acting Owner is told; other Owners of the workspace are not (the API holds no list of their addresses).
6. **The business profile** (`GET`/`PATCH /v1/tenants/{t}/workspace/profile`): name, business type, city, phone, languages, logo. Only the OWNER writes it, with NO second factor asked (fixing a name is not a risky act). The name is the workspace's own row: everything that renders it (greeting, previews, call and WhatsApp openings, the menu header) reads it live; the voice preview's cache is keyed by the spoken text, so a renamed business is heard under the new name at once. Audited (`workspace.business_saved`: the keys changed, never the values).
7. **The business type** is a pack. "Other" is the new data-only pack `general_business` ("Other business", a generic Lead Desk template; the pack id `other` would collide with the word in every file the pack-id lint scans). The API takes `type: "other"` plus `type_label` (2 to 60 characters, the Owner's own words) and maps it in the database. A change inside the Lead Desk types and Other is free. **Out of a deep pack** (one whose modules include the catalogue, orders or fees) only when none of its records exist; **into a deep pack** only when the business has no leads (its screens would not show them). Otherwise 409 `type_change_refused` with a closed `reason` (`pack_data_present`, `lead_data_present`, `unknown_type`; SQLSTATE `SM791`). This departs from "moving between packs is an operator act" (ADR 0084) by the owner's decision; the source of the choice is `owner` in `packs.workspace_pack`.
8. **Members' own profile and pictures:** `workspace.member_profiles` (name, language, picture) and a private per-business bucket `img-<tenant>` with storage rules: every member reads it, the Owner and Admin put the logo, every member puts and removes THEIR OWN picture (`member/<their id>/…`). The API re-draws every upload server side (Pillow: JPEG, at most 1280 px for the logo and 320 px for a picture, all metadata dropped, 5 MB and 40 megapixels at most, jpeg/png/webp only). The Google photo is the default: a Google address (`*.googleusercontent.com`) read from the sign-in, never another host.
9. **The voice preview** (`GET /ai-assistant/voice-preview`, `/text`): the opening line with the workspace's REAL assistant and business names, then one reply (Tamil and Malayalam: the opening only, until a native reader has written the reply), in the chosen language and way of sounding; same cache, six a minute and daily character cap as the listening sample; never a recording.

## Consequences
- pgTAP: `tests.as_aal('aal1')` and `'absent'` stay the old tests' password-only session of a person WHO HAS an authenticator: the helper gives that identity a verified factor (rolled back with the test), so every old "refused at aal1" test keeps its meaning; `tests.as_no_factor(true)` makes the person one without. Test 130 covers the new rules (no window, once per session); test 47 starts from no factors before it enrols its own.
- Tests that counted the packs (twelve) now count thirteen.
- Open for the owner: the real Telugu/Hindi/Kannada reply lines of the voice preview (hi and kn are drafts); a native review of the `general_business` words.
