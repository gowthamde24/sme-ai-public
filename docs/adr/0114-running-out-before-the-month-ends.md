# ADR 0114: running out before the month ends: alerts, top-ups, auto top-up, upgrade, overage, queued calls, invoices (job DS part E2, night 4)

Status: built on branch `api/ds-topups` (27 Oct 2026), based on `api/night4-combined`. Source: the owner's decisions of 26 Oct (night4-extra.md): alert at 80 and 100 percent with a projected run-out date, top-up packs, optional auto top-up, mid-month upgrade with pro-rata, optional pay-per-minute overage with a monthly cap, and when nothing is bought: calls pause and queue, leads are still captured, free WhatsApp replies inside 24 hours continue. AI itself stays "included, fair use". **Payments are fake and billing stays off until the owner's gates** (`billing_enforced`).

## Decision
1. **Extend job DI's meter, do not replace it.** `app.billing_meter` is renamed `billing_meter_base` and wrapped; `billing_allowance`, `billing_stopped` and `billing_record` are re-created under the same names. The operator's `billing_topups` ledger keeps working exactly as before. Meter state keeps its three words; new fields are additive (`blocked`, `queued`, `topup_balance`, `next_topup_expiry`, `projected_run_out_on`, `overage`).
2. **Packs are data** (`billing.topup_packs`, `billing.topup_config`); prices are placeholders (`price_is_placeholder`) until the owner of the product sets them; plans.json is not touched. A purchase is idempotent on a client-chosen id.
3. **Consumption order by recorded draws:** the plan's allowance first, then the oldest unexpired pack, one `billing_topup_draws` row per pack per recording, so a pack's remaining units are always `units - draws` and nothing is estimated. Packs expire 90 days after purchase; unused units carry across periods.
4. **Auto top-up and overage are OFF by default and each needs a monthly cap above zero.** Auto top-up buys at the moment a kind is used up if the month's automatic purchases plus the pack (GST included) stay within the cap and the plan has a payment method on file; the purchase id is derived from (workspace, kind, period, n) so it cannot repeat. Overage covers units beyond all packs at the plan's pay-as-you-go rate (or the placeholder rate) until the cap; the period's overage is invoiced once, after it ended, by the worker.
5. **A used-up kind blocks only when billing is ON.** `app.billing_blocked` is the pure test (used up and no overage open); `billing_stopped` adds the platform switch. Calls that cannot start become `waiting_for_minutes` items (queued, not lost) and come back by themselves (top-up, upgrade, settings change, next period). A connected outreach call now records its seconds on the meter.
6. **Invoices are fake records with GST in whole paise:** taxable (GST-exclusive) + 18 percent, half-up; CGST + SGST when the buyer's state (first two digits of the workspace GSTIN) equals the seller's, IGST otherwise or when there is no GSTIN. The seller (name, state 36) is a placeholder in `billing.topup_config`.
7. **Alerts** use the existing notice (once per kind, level, period) and are delivered by the worker on two channels, e-mail (the app's one sender) and a fake WhatsApp message to the owner; each channel is marked after it went. Today shows `topup_low` / `topup_empty` to the Owner and Admin. The 80 percent alert is dropped once the 100 percent one exists. All of it only while billing is ON and never for an exempt workspace.
8. **Mid-month upgrade:** pro-rata = (new monthly price - old) x days left / days in the period, half up, from the plan catalogue; the new plan's allowance applies at once and the period does not move; during the trial (or while a plan waits for WhatsApp) nothing is charged. With prices unset the upgrade is allowed with a zero-rupee test invoice.

## Consequences
- Tests 111 (E17: callers of `billing_stopped`) and 113 (D5) are unchanged in meaning; the new code reads `billing_blocked`. Test 79 counts three more scheduler functions (42).
- The projected run-out is a straight-line pace over the days elapsed in the period; it says nothing about a sudden campaign.
- A real payment provider changes only `BillingProvider.charge_once` / `change_plan` and the browser's checkout; the database rules do not change.
- Open for the owner: the real prices and rates, the seller's name, state and GSTIN, the owner's WhatsApp number and template, a language per owner, and whether the monthly overage cap should be per kind.
