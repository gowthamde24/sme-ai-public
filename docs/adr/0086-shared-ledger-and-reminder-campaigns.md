# ADR 0086: the shared receivables ledger and reminder campaigns: entries, receipts, an approval for the whole campaign, the worker, the call list and the results

Status: **ACCEPTED and BUILT (job CP, 2026-10-14), for the owner's review.** Builds on ADR 0084 parts 1 to 3 (packs and modules; reminder campaigns), 0085 (the dues groundwork this generalises), 0066 (the outbox and its policy), 0068 (the scheduler), 0014 (erasure) and the connectors' call bridge (ADR 0080). The web screens are jobs CQ (campaigns, call list, results) and CS (fees); education's fee plans and students are job CR. Nothing here is deployed, no paid service or dependency was added, and all data is synthetic.

**The owner's decision on the campaign money rule is recorded below as ACCEPTED, pending the lawyer's confirmation.** Until the lawyer has answered, no workspace may be opened to real customer data for a campaign with amounts (see "The money rule").

## Context

Job CN gave construction customers a dues groundwork (invoices, payments, FIFO allocation) keyed to a company. Education (job CR) needs the same engine for a guardian's fees, with instalments, concessions and receipts. And both packs need to remind people about what is due, without a person approving every message: approving 300 fee reminders one by one is not workable, and the outbox holds any message with money words for a person (ADR 0066). The rule of the whole product is unchanged: **a model never calculates a price or an amount**.

## Decisions

### The ledger

1. **One ledger for every pack.** `ledger_accounts` (a party is a **company** or a **contact**, one account each) and `ledger_entries` replace CN's `customer_invoices` and `customer_payments` (copied with their ids, then dropped). An entry is a **charge** (an invoice, a fee instalment: it has a **due date**, and the owner's invoice number, unique among live charges, or a short label such as a fee head), a **payment** (a method and a **receipt number**), an **adjustment** (a debit with a due date, such as a late fee, or a credit; always a reason code) or a **concession** (a credit with a reason code). Entries never change and are never deleted: a mistake is **voided**, once. CN's functions keep their names, arguments and answers (`invoice_record`, `payment_record`, `dues_statement`, `dues_summary`, `credit_check`, ...), so the construction flow and its 103 checks did not change; new ones (`ledger_entry_record`, `ledger_statement`, `ledger_summary`, `ledger_entries_list`, `ledger_receipt_get`, `ledger_charges_import`) serve a company or a contact.
2. **The allocation rule lives in one place** (`app.ledger_open_items`): credits (payments, concessions, credit adjustments) are applied to the **oldest claim first** (entry date, then due date, then id). A party who has paid more than was invoiced shows a **credit**, never a negative balance. The statement gives balance, credit, age since the entry (0-30, 31-60, 61-90, 90+) **and days past due** (not due, 1-30, 31-60, 61-90, 90+), and what is overdue.
3. **Receipts are numbered per business.** `R/2026-27/000001`: one series per business per Indian financial year (April to March), numbered in the order receipts are made, under an advisory lock; a voided receipt keeps its number and a number is never reused (a unique index refuses a duplicate even from a privileged session). A replay returns the same number and takes no new one.
4. **Who may do what.** Owner, Admin and Sales record charges and payments; **adjustments and concessions** change what is owed without money coming in, so they are the Owner's or Admin's **with a second factor**; Owner and Admin void; a Viewer reads no money. The module is `dues`, which the construction pack has now and the education pack has already (so CR needs no new gate). The credit limit stays as CN built it, keyed by company.
5. **Amounts come only from entries and imports a person made.** Never from a model. A fee list or an accounting export is imported by `ledger_charges_import` (up to 5,000 rows, in parts of 500, derived ids so a retry adds nothing, a row that cannot be added is reported, never fatal).

### Campaigns

6. **Reminders only** (ADR 0084 part 3): kinds `payment_due`, `delivery_update`, `fee_due`, `event`, `exam`, `holiday`. Each kind needs its own module besides `campaigns` (payment_due: `dues`, delivery_update: `deliveries`, fee_due: `fees`, event/exam/holiday: `programmes`), so a construction business sees payment due and delivery updates, an education business sees fee due, events, exams and holidays (and payment due, because it has `dues`), and **a textile business sees none** (`campaigns` is not in its pack). Nothing reads a pack id. Only the money kinds carry an amount; the others refuse `{{amount}}`, an amount on a recipient, and any money word.
7. **A campaign is data.** A list of people the business already deals with; one **approved WhatsApp template** (purpose `reminder`, status `approved`, the campaign's language); **one fixed text** with five merge fields (`{{name}}`, `{{amount}}`, `{{due_date}}`, `{{reference}}`, `{{business}}`: no other braces, no link, one line, no promise or complaint word; a money kind may hold money words only); and **up to five steps** that are **offsets from each person's own date** (days -60 to +180, time of day in India time) inside the business's allowed hours. The text is filled from the recipient's own values by a pure SQL function; nothing else is ever substituted.
8. **The list** comes from an **import** (rows by contact id or phone number: the number only *finds* an existing contact and is not stored; a repeat adds nothing; at most 5,000 people; an amount is at most Rs 10 lakh a message) or from the **ledger** (`campaign_recipients_from_ledger`: everyone with money open on claims due in a date range; one recipient per account, the amount the OPEN total of those claims, the date the earliest due date; a company's reminder goes to its first contact with a number). Such a recipient is tied to its account, so **the worker checks the ledger again before each message**: paid since is skipped (`paid`), paid in part is skipped (`balance_changed`): a person is never reminded of an amount they no longer owe. An outbox message hangs on a lead; a contact of a company with no lead gets one made, marked `source = 'campaign'`; a contact with no company cannot be sent to (`no_lead`): education must link a guardian to a company (the institution) or lift that lead rule: **open item for job CR**.
9. **Every message is an ordinary outbox message**, category `campaign_reminder`, source `system`, with an id **derived from (campaign, person, step, due date)**: a retry or a second worker cannot send twice, and the same id with other words conflicts. STOP, consent or contact history, the suppression key, quiet hours, the daily limit, the kill switch and the business's automatic sending are checked **per message** by the outbox's own rules. The first message to a person carries the AI-disclosure line like any other. The one difference, stated by the Owner's decision: **the money words of an approved campaign do not hold the message for a person**. A name that reads as a promise or a complaint still does (reason `commitment` / `complaint`, held for a person).
10. **The WhatsApp template** is the campaign's own (`app.wa_send_context` names the campaign's template, and only if it is still `approved` in the campaign's language); outside the 24-hour window the adapter needs it. The template's one variable carries the rendered text.

### The money rule (owner decision, ACCEPTED pending the lawyer)

11. ADR 0066 holds a message with money words for a person. For a **campaign**, the **Owner, with a second factor, approves the WHOLE campaign once**, after seeing **rendered samples**: the **first** person, the one with the **longest** message, the one with the **highest amount** and **three at random** (chosen from the content hash, so the same list shows the same three; a person who is two of these appears once). The approval **binds to a hash of the template's words, the steps, the call plan, the template and every row of the list, and to the hash of the samples that were seen**: the Owner passes back the hash of what they saw, and if the campaign changed since, the approval is refused (409 `samples_changed`). **Any change afterwards sends the campaign back to draft** (a new word, a step, a recipient, the template, the language): the approval is cleared, the planned steps are cancelled, the audit trail says why; a new name alone is not content. The worker also recomputes the hash before each step and, if it differs (someone changed a row behind the back of the functions), **sends nothing and sends the campaign back to draft**. The approval only plans steps; **nothing is sent by it**. A step whose time is already past at approval is recorded as skipped (`past`), never sent in a burst; a step more than a day late (a worker that was down, a campaign paused for days) is skipped (`missed`). The amount is a number from the list or the ledger, in a fixed slot of a fixed text, capped at Rs 10 lakh a message.
12. **STATUS: ACCEPTED by the owner (2026-10-14), PENDING the lawyer's confirmation.** This changes ADR 0066's rule for one class of message. What it needs from the lawyer: that a fixed, owner-approved reminder with an amount from the owner's own list is acceptable as a service/utility message to a customer the business already deals with; the wording of the AI-disclosure line for a bulk reminder; whether a reminder to an existing customer needs more than the consent history the outbox already requires. **Until then**: the real-data gate (ADR 0015) stays closed for every workspace, so no real contact, and so no real campaign, can exist; opening the gate for a workspace that will run a campaign with amounts is gated on this answer (`docs/pre-pilot-checklist.md`, "Reminder campaigns"). Nothing in code can loosen the rule: the money kinds still need the Owner's second factor and the hash of what they saw.
13. **What is NOT built, and why.** ADR 0084's second owner decision, a recorded **attestation** that an imported list gave their number, is **not** built: it is a legal act the lawyer must word. Until then a list of people the business has no opt-in or history with is blocked honestly, message by message (`no_opt_in`) and the results say so. Per-step wording (one text per campaign is built; a text that reads well before and after the date uses the date merge field), several languages in one campaign (one language per campaign; a second language is a second campaign), pacing of sends beyond the outbox's batches (Meta's per-number limits are the provider's question), and a promotional broadcast (excluded).

### The call list

14. A campaign may say "after step N, wait H hours (default 24, at most 168)". For each person whose step N was handed over at least H hours ago and **was sent or failed**, who has **not replied on WhatsApp since**, has **not paid** (a ledger list is checked again; a payer is closed as paid) and **can still be called** (not erased, not stopped, has a number, phone consent not withdrawn), the worker makes a **call request** and an **item on the call list**, one per person. **Deviation from ADR 0084's third recommendation**: the call request is **approved with the campaign**, by the Owner who approved it (decision note "approved with the campaign"), because the Owner approved the whole campaign including this step with their second factor, and a request per person would make a 300-person campaign unusable. Nothing dials by itself: staff call through the **existing bridge** (Owner and Admin start it; it still refuses outside 09:00 to 21:00 India time, for a stopped or erased contact, and without a number), and the list **never shows the number**.
15. Staff record the **outcome** (Owner, Admin, Sales): **paid** (the person is closed; the rest of their reminders are skipped), **promised** with a date from today to 60 days (the reminders due before the day after the promise are skipped; **later ones still go**), **call later** with a time within 30 days (the item stays open), **wrong number** (closed), **stop** (the contact is put on the do-not-contact list, for every campaign and every message). The same outcome recorded again replays; another outcome on a finished item is refused.

### The worker and the results

16. `scheduler_campaign_run` is a function of the **scheduler's own principal** (job AW) and acts only when the global outbox switch AND the business's automatic sending are on and the module is on. The tick has a new phase between the visit reminders and the release (`app/scheduler/tick.py`): one call per business, counts only, each step in its own sub-transaction so one odd step cannot hold back the others. A campaign is **done** when nothing is planned, no call is open and a call plan's waiting hours (plus two days) have passed. Pause, resume and cancel are the Owner's or Admin's.
17. **Results are derived**, nothing is stored: the state of every step is read from the outbox and the WhatsApp status table (planned, skipped with a reason, held, waiting, sent, delivered, read, failed, blocked with a reason), counted by state, by reason and by India day, plus recipients who **replied** (wrote on WhatsApp after the first reminder reached them), who **paid after a reminder** (closed as paid, or a ledger list that is now settled and was open on the day of the first reminder), the recipients closed and why, and the call list's counts and outcomes.

## Privacy and erasure

The ledger holds no personal data of its own: amounts, dates, closed words, a strict-pattern label; an account points to a company or a contact, and erasing the party anonymises them while the money records stay (accounting records). A campaign's **name and text** are the owner's words: classified personal (the owner can type a name), erased by the tenant scope and swept for exact identifiers. A recipient's label (`reference`) is a strict-pattern label (no name, no number). The messages are outbox messages and are erased like any other. A model never reads these tables, and never sees a name, an amount or a text of a campaign. A child's data is not here: education's student record is job CR's, and a student label would travel as the recipient's `reference`, which CR must decide.

## Deviations and risks

* **The call request is approved with the campaign** (point 14): a deliberate departure from ADR 0084's recommendation, for the owner to confirm.
* **A campaign's text is one text for every step**; per-step wording and several languages per campaign are not built.
* **An imported list is not tied to the ledger**; only a list made *from* the ledger is checked against it before each message. A paid person on an imported list is stopped by the call outcome, not by the ledger.
* **The money rule is not yet the lawyer's** (point 12).
* **WhatsApp quality** (a bulk sender can damage the business's rating and number) is the provider's and Meta's question; the outbox's daily limit and the per-business batches are the only pacing.
* **A guardian with no company** has no lead to send on (point 8): job CR must link guardians to a company or change that rule.
* Mutation pass not run (opt-in, about 45 minutes; nothing here touches the follow-up engine).
