# ADR 0102: the assistant is in Today's card, a floating orb elsewhere, and the menu has seven items (job DQ, part 0)

Status: built on branch `web/dq-today-ask` (night run 27 Oct 2026). Replaces the Ask dock of job DN and the bottom bar of job DM. Owner feedback of 26 Oct: "the assistant in the middle does nothing, we can't type anything. Then why do we have a search bar at the bottom?"

## Context
The question line of Today (`AskTeam`, `hero` variant) was a bar fixed to the bottom of the screen, so the assistant's card in the middle had no input at all, and job DN put the same bar at the bottom of the other screens (the "dock"). The menu had twenty-odd rows in four groups.

## Decision
1. **One conversation per workspace.** The session (question, streaming answer, sources and drafts, the microphone, the spoken answer, the thread of earlier exchanges) is `useAskSession`; it runs once, in `AskSessionHost`, which the workspace layout mounts through `AskProvider` and hands to a tiny store (`ask-store.ts`). Today's card (`AskCard`) and the floating panel read the same session, so a question asked on Leads is on Today, with "Show earlier (n)" for the exchanges before it. The thread is in memory only: nothing of an answer is stored in the browser. A hard reload starts a new thread.
2. **Today:** the line (text, microphone, Send), the three chips and the latest exchange are INSIDE the assistant's card. Nothing is fixed to the screen. **Every other screen:** a 56 px orb at the bottom right (breathing, thinking, speaking; a dot when an answer arrived unseen) opens a dialog: about 380 by 560 on a laptop, a sheet 70 % high on a phone, with the chips of that screen (`ask-chips.ts`). Esc and the close button return to the orb; the focus is trapped inside; "/" and Cmd or Ctrl+K open it (on Today they focus the card's line). No orb on Today, on the live call, in a read-only workspace, or when there is nothing to ask. The bottom bar and the dock are gone.
3. **The code is not on every page:** the layout would otherwise carry the whole session. `AskSessionHost` and the panel are their own chunks (`next/dynamic`); the layout holds the orb, the store and the chips table. Route budgets stay green without a ceiling raised.
4. **The menu is seven items** for every business type: Today, Customers, Chats, Calls, Sales, Catalogue, Reports, with Settings below them (`MENU` in `nav.ts`). Each item is a group of the pages that existed (`members`), shown as tabs above the page (`GroupTabs`) when the person's role and the business type offer more than one. NO page and NO address moved: a tab is a plain link, so nothing needs a redirect. Team, Office and Integrations are tabs of Settings. The phone's bar is Today, Customers, Chats and Sales, then More (Calls, Catalogue, Reports, Settings); the bar says "Sales" for every business type (the business type's own word for its core work is the tab inside).
5. **Wording.** A demo workspace (its name starts with "DEMO") says "Demo workspace: sending is switched off. Nothing goes out; you can still read and edit messages." instead of "Sending is stopped ... until the operator ...". The word "operator" is no longer shown to an owner anywhere ("our team"). "Your team" on Today is a list of rows, not three narrow columns.

## Consequences
- The chips are asked in the person's own language (the old Today card asked two of them in English); C1's evals cover the words (c2-questions-3.md).
- The API does not say which workspace is a demo; the name is the hint until it does.
- The e2e script `e2e/dq-ask-shots.mjs` checks the owner's list in a real browser; `e2e/dn-shots.mjs` follows the orb instead of the dock. The visual baselines change (no bottom bar, the new menu) and are rebuilt with the night run.
